## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Volaris Flies DFW–SLP Daily

**CLAIM:** "Volaris and American fly DFW–SLP daily in about 2h 19m" / "Volaris DFW↔SLP: daily nonstop"

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
American is daily; Volaris is not. From 29 March 2026 onward, Volaris Y47912 is scheduled Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun (four weekly), not seven. Dallas Fort Worth Airport’s Volaris destinations page lists San Luis Potosí as Monday, Friday, Sunday in its current summary. Block time is about 2h 10m–2h 15m, close to the article’s 2h 19m, but the “daily” frequency claim is wrong.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [FlightMapper — Volaris Y47912 DFW→SLP](https://info.flightmapper.net/flight/Volaris_Y4_7912) — Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun from 2026-03-29; duration 2:10–2:15
- **Corroborating Source:** [DFW Airport — Volaris destinations](https://www.airport-dallas.com/dfw-airlines/volaris) — SLP listed Mon/Fri/Sun
- **Corroborating Source:** [OMA — Volaris Texas routes launch](https://news.oma.aero/news/san-luis-potosi-enhances-connectivity-with-new-international-routes-to-dallas-houston-and-san-antonio-71125-f8d04.html) — DFW launched at four weekly (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article repeatedly pairs Volaris with American under a single “daily” label. Only American matches that pattern in September 2026. Calling Volaris daily overstates capacity by roughly 40–50% versus a four-day schedule and misleads tournament planners booking around match days that may fall on Volaris off-days (Tue/Thu/Sat).

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent schedule databases agree Volaris is not daily.

### CLAIM 2: American Airlines DFW–SLP Daily Nonstop (~2h 19m)

**CLAIM:** "American Airlines DFW↔SLP: daily nonstop, ~2h 19m"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
American operates daily DFW–SLP nonstops in September 2026 (AA1440 from 9 September 2026 at 09:59, block time about 2h 10m). Published block times across 2026 seasons range from about 2h 05m to 2h 25m, so ~2h 19m is a fair mid-range description even if current September blocks are often closer to 2h 10m. A second daily AA frequency (evening AA861/AA478) appears seasonally in other windows but is not required for the “daily” claim to hold.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [FlightMapper — American Airlines DFW–SLP](http://info.flightmapper.net/route/American_Airlines_AA_DFW_SLP) — AA1440 daily effective from 2026-09-09; 2:05–2:25 across seasons
- **Corroborating Source:** [DirectFlights — DFW–SLP](https://www.directflights.com/DFW-SLP) — American + Volaris nonstops; American ~7/week
- **Corroborating Source:** [American Airlines — DFW to SLP fares](https://www.aa.com/en-us/flights-from-dallas-to-san-luis-potosi) — Live booking inventory for the route in Aug–Nov 2026

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Core claim (daily AA nonstop DFW–SLP with ~2-hour-plus block time) is confirmed. No evidence of suspension on this mainline route as of 21 September 2026.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Airline schedule aggregators and AA’s own fare page confirm active daily service.

### CLAIM 3: United IAH–SLP About 14× Weekly (~2h 1m)

**CLAIM:** "United flies IAH–SLP about 14 times weekly in about 2h 1m" / "United IAH↔SLP: ~14 weekly nonstops, ~2h 1m"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
For the August–October 2026 window that includes late September, United schedules two daily IAH→SLP departures (morning UA5945/UA646 family and evening UA5283), i.e. 14 weekly. Published block times run roughly 1h 51m–2h 16m; ~2h 1m sits inside that band. The route is active and not among the Mexican-carrier U.S. routes revoked in the October 2025 DOT order (those hit NLU/MEX growth, not United’s SLP service).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [FlightMapper — IAH–SLP](https://info.flightmapper.net/route/YY_IAH_SLP) — Dual daily United pattern Aug 18–Oct 24 2026 (UA5945 + UA5283)
- **Corroborating Source:** [FlightsFrom — IAH–SLP](https://www.flightsfrom.com/IAH-SLP) — United nonstops; ~2 flights/day pattern in seasonal calendars
- **Corroborating Source:** [AirportOverview — IAH–SLP](https://www.airportoverview.com/routes/IAH/SLP) — United listed at ~21/week in some snapshots when a third frequency is present; article’s ~14 remains accurate for the two-daily period

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Frequency is seasonal: winter/shoulder banks can drop below or rise above 14. As of verification week (21–27 September 2026), dual daily ≈14 weekly is correct. Duration claim is acceptable.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Schedule banks for mid/late 2026 are explicit on FlightMapper.

### CLAIM 4: Volaris Off-Peak Fares From About $81–126 USD

**CLAIM:** "cheapest listed fares start at $81–126 on Volaris" / "Volaris off-peak: $81–126 USD one-way"

**VERDICT:** 🔄 OUTDATED

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
On 21 September 2026, Volaris’s own DFW–SLP fare page showed September one-ways from about USD167 and October from about USD120; the site’s broader “from” teaser reached USD112 (January 2027). The $81–126 floor quoted in the April 2026 article is no longer what Volaris displays for near-term DFW–SLP travel. Nearby SAT–SLP still teases lower floors (e.g. from ~USD79 in October), which may explain older marketing lows, but it does not rescue the DFW claim.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Volaris — Flights from Dallas to San Luis Potosi](https://www.volaris.com/en-us/flights-from-dallas-to-san-luis-potosi) — Sep 2026 from USD167; Oct–Nov from USD120; Jan 2027 from USD112 (viewed 21 Sep 2026)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Volaris — SAT to SLP](https://www.volaris.com/en-us/flights-from-san-antonio-to-san-luis-potosi) — Lower teaser fares on the San Antonio nonstop, not DFW
- **Corroborating Source:** [American Airlines — DFW to SLP](https://www.aa.com/en-us/flights-from-dallas-to-san-luis-potosi) — Round-trips from ~$498 in Aug/Sep 2026 (mainline prices sit above Volaris, as the article suggests directionally)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
ULCC fares move constantly; treating a spring 2026 low as a standing “off-peak” floor for a September 2026 reader is outdated. Tournament-window premiums ($200–350) remain plausible but were not independently locked to a fixed bookable sample for every date.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Direct read of the carrier’s fare page on verification day.

### CLAIM 5: Volaris Launched DFW–SLP in July 2025

**CLAIM:** "Volaris launched the DFW–SLP route in July 2025 and remains the cheapest year-round option."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
OMA and Volaris announced SLP–DFW service starting 4 July 2025 at four weekly frequencies, with contemporaneous inauguration coverage. The “cheapest year-round” half is directionally supported by September 2026 Volaris vs American displayed fares, though absolute floors change.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [OMA news — new SLP routes to Dallas, Houston, San Antonio](https://news.oma.aero/news/san-luis-potosi-enhances-connectivity-with-new-international-routes-to-dallas-houston-and-san-antonio-71125-f8d04.html) — DFW from 4 July 2025, four weekly
- **Corroborating Source:** [EspacioAéreo — Volaris ribbon-cutting](https://espacioaereo.net/archivos/51481) — July 2025 inauguration of SLP–Dallas leg
- **Corroborating Source:** [Nitu / DFW route note](https://nitu.mx/index.php/2025/07/28/el-aeropuerto-internacional-dfw-suma-dos-nuevas-rutas-estrategicas-con-volaris-desde-el-occidente-de-mexico/) — SLP–DFW Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun schedule at launch

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Launch month and carrier are correct. “Cheapest year-round” is a soft comparative that held on verification samples but should be read as marketing language, not a guaranteed forever floor.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Airport-operator primary press release.

### CLAIM 6: “The Three Direct Routes” Are Only DFW (Volaris/AA) and IAH (United)

**CLAIM:** Table and framing list three Texas nonstops — Volaris DFW, American DFW, United IAH — as the complete direct picture for Texas travelers.

**VERDICT:** 🔀 MISLEADING

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Those three services exist, but the article’s Texas→SLP map is incomplete. Volaris also flies nonstop SAT–SLP (launched 5 July 2025, still operating in August–September 2026 booking/status data) and IAH–SLP (~3 weekly). Austin (AUS) has no nonstop to SLP. Aeroméxico and Viva Aerobus do not operate Texas–SLP nonstops; Viva’s SLP growth has been domestic (e.g. NLU/MTY). No evidence that the Texas–SLP Volaris/AA/United routes were suspended as of verification date.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [OMA — SAT/IAH/DFW launch package](https://news.oma.aero/news/san-luis-potosi-enhances-connectivity-with-new-international-routes-to-dallas-houston-and-san-antonio-71125-f8d04.html) — SAT three weekly from 5 July 2025
- **Corroborating Source:** [Volaris — SAT to SLP fares](https://www.volaris.com/en-us/flights-from-san-antonio-to-san-luis-potosi) — Bookable Sep–Oct 2026 inventory
- **Corroborating Source:** [Avionio — Y47961 SLP→SAT history](https://www.avionio.com/en/flight/y4-7961) — Multiple August 2026 operated/landed flights
- **Corroborating Source:** [Kayak / Travelmath — AUS–SLP](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Austin-Bergstrom-AUS/San-Luis-Potosi-SLP) — No nonstop AUS–SLP
- **Counter-evidence (suspensions elsewhere):** [USDOT — Mexican carrier route disapprovals](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-slashes-13-mexican-carrier-routes-us) — NLU/MEX dispute routes; not SLP–Texas mainline service

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Framing “Dallas or Houston” and a three-row table as the full Texas nonstop set understates San Antonio and Volaris Houston. That omission matters for fare shopping (SAT often prices below DFW on Volaris) and for readers in South/Central Texas. AUS correctly has no nonstop.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Launch PR plus live Volaris inventory and flight-history records.

### CLAIM 7: SLP Airport Is About 25 km North of the City

**CLAIM:** "Aeropuerto Internacional Ponciano Arriaga (SLP) Location: ~25 km north of San Luis Potosí city."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The airport sits on Carretera a Matehuala Km 9.5, north of the urban core. Independent aviation guides place it about 15 km from downtown; San Luis Way’s own later airport guide uses 15–20 km. ~25 km overstates the common Centro figure by several kilometers though direction and “north of city” are correct.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Aeropuertos.Net — Aeropuerto de San Luis Potosí](https://www.aeropuertos.net/aeropuerto-de-san-luis-potosi/) — ~15 km from urban center; Carr. a Matehuala Km 9.5
- **Corroborating Source:** [San Luis Way — SLP Airport Guide](https://www.sanluisway.com/blog/san-luis-potosi-airport-guide) — 15–20 km / 20–30 minutes to Centro

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Identity (Ponciano Arriaga / SLP) is correct. Distance should be revised to ~15–20 km for Centro/Lomas trips.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Consistent geographic references.

### CLAIM 8: Authorized Airport Taxi Costs 350–450 MXN ($20–26 USD)

**CLAIM:** "Authorized taxi (fixed rate): 350–450 MXN ($20–26 USD) to Centro Histórico or Lomas."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Published 2026 special taxi tables reported by Astrolabio put the Terminal Terrestre↔airport special fare at MX$275 (up from 266 in 2025 and 255 in 2024). San Luis Way’s July 2026 airport guide — after its own fact-check — quotes MX$275 to Centro Histórico at the authorized arrivals counter. The 350–450 MXN band is materially high versus the documented fixed-rate figure.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Astrolabio — 2026 taxi tariffs](https://www.astrolabio.com.mx/taxis-en-slp-el-banderazo-sube-hasta-16-80-pesos-en-2026/) — Airport special fare 275 MXN in 2026
- **Corroborating Source:** [San Luis Way — SLP Airport Guide](https://www.sanluisway.com/blog/san-luis-potosi-airport-guide) — MX$275 authorized taxi to Centro (verified July 2026)
- **Corroborating Source:** [OMA SLP — Taxis page](https://aeropuertosanluispotosi.oma.aero/es/estacionamento-y-transporte/taxis.php) — Official taxi information portal at the airport

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Rideshare quotes (article’s Uber/DiDi 200–280 MXN) can still float with demand, but the authorized fixed-rate window claim is contradicted by the 275 MXN published special fare. USD conversion in the article also overstates a 275 MXN ticket at mid-2026 FX.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Local fare reporting plus prior SLW verification of the same figure.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Carrier identity for the core Dallas and Houston nonstops is mostly right (American daily; United ~14 weekly; Volaris present since July 2025). Errors cluster on Volaris frequency, fare floors, route completeness (SAT/IAH Volaris omitted), and ground-transfer prices.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Strong tournament-travel framing pushes SLP as the “cleanest” Texas entry versus CDMX hotels; hotel-premium percentages and “book by April 30” advice were not independently verified here. Schedule claims are presented as settled 2026 facts despite clear seasonality.

### SOURCING QUALITY
The live article does not cite OMA, airline schedule pages, or fare timestamps. Independent verification required FlightMapper, airline sites, OMA press, Astrolabio, and AUS/SAT route checkers.
