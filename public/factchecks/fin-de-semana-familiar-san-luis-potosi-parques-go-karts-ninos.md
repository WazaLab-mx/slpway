## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Tangamanga I second-largest urban park with free zoo and playgrounds

**CLAIM:** "Start your adventure at Mexico's second-largest urban park" / "Parque Tangamanga I - Zoo & Playgrounds … Cost: FREE" / "Tangamanga Park has a small zoo, pedal boats, and playgrounds all for free."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Official state tourism copy still presents Tangamanga I as Mexico’s second-largest urban park after Chapultepec (~420 ha). Park entry and playgrounds remain free. The UMA zoo, however, is listed by CECURT as temporarily closed for remodeling, with local coverage through 2025–2026 describing ongoing rehabilitation—so a free zoo visit is not currently available as advertised.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Gobierno SLP — Parque Tangamanga I](https://slp.gob.mx/lugares/parque-tangamanga-1/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [CECURT FAQ — Zoológico UMA temporalmente cerrado](https://cecurt.slp.gob.mx/faq/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Orquesta — UMA remodelación / cerrada al público](https://laorquesta.mx/usuarios-encerrados-animales-libres-nueva-estrategia-en-los-tangamanga/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The ranking claim matches Tier-1 tourism language. Free access to the park and playgrounds is consistent with operator practice. Treating the zoo as an open free highlight is no longer accurate; CECURT’s own FAQ is the decisive current status. Pedal boats were not re-verified for this report.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Official park pages and CECURT FAQ align on ranking and zoo closure.

### CLAIM 2: GoKartMania / Pista SLP races cost $150–250 MXN

**CLAIM:** "GoKartMania/Pista SLP at $150-250 per race" / "Go-Karts at Pista SLP … Cost: $150-250 MXN per person per race"

**VERDICT:** 🔄 OUTDATED

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
GoKartMania Citadella still operates in San Luis Potosí. Its live services page lists a single adult race at $349 MXN (packages lower the unit price toward ~$230–$299; Kartito is $150; Baby Kart $50). Kartódromo SLP (often the outdoor “pista”) publishes weekday sessions from about $220–$349+. The article’s $150–250 band no longer matches standard adult race pricing.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [GoKartMania Citadella — Precios y paquetes](https://gokartmaniaslp.com/servicios/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Kartódromo SLP — Promociones](https://kartodromoslp.com.mx/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — dos pistas de karts (histórico ~$299 / desde ~$189)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/dos-sitios-para-sentir-la-adrenalina-de-conducir-un-auto-de-carreras-en-la-capital-e-slp/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Both major kart venues still exist. $150 appears only for Kartito or special promos (e.g., past Happy Hour wording), not as the normal adult race range. Conflating “GoKartMania” with “Pista SLP” also blurs two different operators and price sheets.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Operator price tables are primary and current.

### CLAIM 3: Go-kart tracks are on the road to La Pila

**CLAIM:** "Go-kart tracks are located on the road to La Pila."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No active go-kart venue was found on the carretera a La Pila. GoKartMania is at Plaza Citadella, Av. Salvador Nava Martínez 3125 (beside Tangamanga I). Kartódromo San Luis / Kartódromo SLP is at Av. Anillo Periférico Norte 2040, Col. Las Flores.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [GoKartMania Citadella — ubicación Plaza Citadella](https://gokartmaniaslp.com/servicios/)
- **Primary Source:** [Kartódromo San Luis — Periférico Nte. 2040](https://www.kartodromosanluis.mx/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — ubicaciones Citadella vs Periférico](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/dos-sitios-para-sentir-la-adrenalina-de-conducir-un-auto-de-carreras-en-la-capital-e-slp/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Searches for karts + La Pila returned only the two known city venues above. The tip would send families to the wrong corridor.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Addresses come from operator sites; contradiction is clear.

### CLAIM 4: Laberinto museum prices $80 adults / $60 kids, inside Tangamanga

**CLAIM:** "Laberinto de las Ciencias y las Artes … Cost: $80 MXN adults, $60 MXN kids" / "Laberinto de las Ciencias is inside Tangamanga Park"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Location is correct: the museum sits inside Parque Tangamanga I (Blvd. Antonio Rocha Cordero). Published general admission on Secretaría de Cultura / SIC listings is about $50–$60 MXN, not an $80 adult / $60 child split. Child discounts appear in secondary listings at lower amounts, but no current primary source matches the article’s $80/$60 pair.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SIC Cultura — Museo Laberinto (entrada general $60; interior Tangamanga area)](https://sic.cultura.gob.mx/ficha.php?table=museo&table_id=1231)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Cultura.gob.mx — Laberinto, precios $50](https://www.cultura.gob.mx/regiones_de_mexico/centro_occidente/detalle_lugar.php?espacio=56438)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Venue still operates and is correctly placed in the itinerary. Ticket figures overstate adult admission relative to federal/cultural directories; treat prices as needing a day-of check at the box office.

**CONFIDENCE:** High for location; Medium for exact current tariffs (directories disagree slightly between $50 and $60).

### CLAIM 5: Bowling at Bol Tanga costs $100–150 MXN per person per game

**CLAIM:** "Bowling at Bol Tanga … Cost: $100-150 MXN per person per game"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Bol Tangamanga / Bolerama Tangamanga still exists in Plaza Gigante Tangamanga (local 31D-4; business registries and directories list phone 444-811-0446). A current official per-game price list matching $100–150 was not found; one consumer directory lists a $300 cost figure of unclear scope. Venue existence is solid; the quoted game price is weakly supported.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Directorio empresarial — BOL TANGAMANGA / Corporativo Kamello, Plaza Tangamanga 31 D 4](https://directorioempresarialmexico.com/empresa/0003716155/BOL-TANGAMANGA/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Y Que Plan — Bolerama Tangamanga (dirección / costo listado $300)](https://yqueplan.com/lugar/bolerama-tangamanga/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Families can still bowl near Tangamanga under this name. Do not rely on the $100–150 band without calling the alley; public price evidence is thin and conflicting.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Registry confirms the business; price band is not corroborated by a primary tariff sheet.

### CLAIM 6: Sunday Ciclovía on Carranza, ~8 AM–1 PM, free

**CLAIM:** "Every Sunday morning, Avenida Venustiano Carranza … closes to cars … It runs from about 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM." / "Cost: FREE"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Local news and 2026 municipal coverage of the Recreovía / paseo dominical consistently place the Carranza car-free window at 8:00–13:00 on Sundays, free of charge. The program remains active (and has been extended along Carranza).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Primicia SLP — Recreovía Dominical Carranza 8:00–13:00 (2026)](https://primiciaslp.com/2026/04/15/recreovia-dominical-del-gobierno-de-la-capital-programa-en-favor-de-todos-los-potosinos/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — Ciclovía Carranza 8:00 a 13:00](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/diversion-dominical-asi-pasan-las-familias-potosinas-su-domingo-en-la-ciclovia-de-carranza/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Hours and free access match independent reporting across years. Bike-rental prices (~$50–100/hr in the article) were not fully re-priced for this claim set.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent local sources, including 2026 city-program coverage.

### CLAIM 7: Rancho La Estación — Matehuala Km 8, $200–400/person, weekends 9 AM–5 PM

**CLAIM:** "Rancho La Estacion … Cost: $200-400 MXN per person … Hours: 9 AM - 5 PM (weekends) … Location: Carretera a Matehuala Km 8 … about 20 minutes from the city center"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The venue still exists at Av. Bicentenario 201 / Carretera a Matehuala Km 8.5, Soledad de Graciano Sánchez. Official site and listings emphasize corporate, social, and school events more than walk-up day tickets. Published hours (e.g., Sat longer, Sun often until mid-afternoon) do not match a flat “9 AM–5 PM weekends,” and no primary source confirmed a $200–400 per-person public package.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Rancho La Estación — Nosotros (Km 8.5 / Av. Bicentenario 201)](https://www.rancholaestacion.com/nosotros)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Especial Evento — horarios publicados Rancho La Estación](https://especialevento.com/san-luis-potosi/soledad-de-graciano-sanchez/organizadoras-de-eventos/rancho-la-estacion/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Location and continued operation are confirmed (~Km 8–8.5 is close enough). Framing it as a simple priced weekend drop-in ranch with fixed 9–5 hours and $200–400 tickets overstates what public sources support; contact the venue for package pricing.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Address is primary; hours/prices for casual family visits lack a clear official tariff.

### CLAIM 8: “La Oruga y la Cebolla” — Mexican-fusion outdoor garden with play area, $200–350/person

**CLAIM:** "La Oruga y la Cebolla Mexican-fusion, outdoor garden with play area Price: $200-350 MXN/person"

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No restaurant named “La Oruga y la Cebolla” was found in San Luis Potosí. The closest active venue is “La Oruga y La Cebada,” an artisan-beer cantina at Av. Universidad 169, Centro Histórico. Its official site describes craft beer and home-style food, not a dedicated kids’ play garden. Naming and kid-play positioning are materially wrong.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Oruga y La Cebada — sitio oficial](https://laorugaylacebada.com.mx/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Rappi / directories — La Oruga y La Cebada, Universidad 169](https://www.rappi.com.mx/restaurantes/1923223541-la-oruga-y-la-cebada)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Web search for the exact “Cebolla” name returns only the “Cebada” cantina. Presenting it as a family play-area restaurant misleads itinerary readers even if per-person spend (~$200+) can overlap casual dining costs.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Official branding and menu contradict the article’s name and play-area claim.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Named major attractions (Tangamanga I, Laberinto, Ciclovía, kart venues, Bol Tangamanga, Rancho La Estación) generally still exist in SLP. Failures cluster on **prices**, **one wrong restaurant name**, **a false La Pila location tip**, and **treating the Tangamanga zoo as open**.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Itinerary marketing bias: optimistic free/cheap framing and blended venue nicknames (“GoKartMania/Pista SLP”) that hide two operators and outdated tariffs.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Strongest on government tourism/CECURT, SIC Cultura, and venue operator sites. Weakest on bowling tariffs and casual ranch day-pass pricing (directories and event listings only).
