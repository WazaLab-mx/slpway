## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: 14th edition in 2026

**CLAIM:** "The International Wine Festival reaches its 14th edition in 2026" / "14th SLP International Wine Festival."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
State tourism listing, the official festival site branding (“14 Festival”), inauguration coverage on May 29, 2026, and multiple local outlets all consistently label the 2026 event as the 14th edition. Post-event Pulso coverage (June 7, 2026) likewise refers to the completed “14ª Edición.”

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Secretaría de Turismo SLP — 14° Festival Internacional del Vino](https://turismo.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/5/7/14-festival-internacional-del-vino/)
- **Primary Source:** [festivaldelvino.mx — official site (14 Festival branding; post-event “Nos vemos en el 2027”)](https://festivaldelvino.mx/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Metropoli San Luis — inauguration of the 14th edition (May 29, 2026)](https://metropolisanluis.com/2026/05/inauguran-la-decimo-cuarta-edicion-del-festival-internacional-del-vino-en-san-luis-potosi/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Organizer Alejandro Espinosa Abaroa has publicly explained that 14 editions span roughly 16 calendar years because the run was interrupted by the pandemic—consistent with calling 2026 the 14th edition rather than a simple “year 16” count. No credible source calls 2026 a different edition number.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — government, official site, and inauguration press agree.

### CLAIM 2: Dates Friday May 29 and Saturday May 30, 2026 (and whether the festival ran)

**CLAIM:** "The 14th San Luis Potosí International Wine Festival takes place Friday, May 29 and Saturday, May 30, 2026."

Those dates were the official program before the event and are confirmed by inauguration and post-event reporting. As of this verification date (September 21, 2026), the festival has already happened successfully; the official site now reads “¡Gracias a ti fue un éxito! Nos vemos en el 2027.”

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Secretaría de Turismo SLP — event listing for May 29–30, 2026 at CEART](https://turismo.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/5/7/14-festival-internacional-del-vino/)

- **Corroborating Source:** [Metropoli San Luis — opened May 29, 2026](https://metropolisanluis.com/2026/05/inauguran-la-decimo-cuarta-edicion-del-festival-internacional-del-vino-en-san-luis-potosi/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The guide was published April 28, 2026 as a forward-looking ticket/logistics piece. Temporal check confirms the announced weekend was not cancelled or moved. Dates are historically accurate; they are not “outdated figures,” only past tense relative to today.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — announcement, inauguration, and wrap-up sources align.

### CLAIM 3: Venue — Centro de las Artes, Calzada de Guadalupe 705

**CLAIM:** "…at the Centro de las Artes (CEART), on Calzada de Guadalupe 705."

Official tourism, venue, and festival materials place the 2026 edition at Centro de las Artes. The street address Calzada de Guadalupe 705 is the venue’s published address on CEART and Cultura SLP listings and matches independent ticket/program coverage.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [CEART San Luis — Calzada de Guadalupe 705](https://ceartsanluis.mx/)
- **Primary Source:** [Cultura SLP — Centro de las Artes address](https://cultura.slp.gob.mx/lugares/centro-de-las-artes-de-san-luis-potosi/)

- **Corroborating Source:** [SIC / Secretaría de Cultura — Calz. de Guadalupe 705](https://sic.cultura.gob.mx/ficha.php?table=centro_cultural&table_id=2065)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Postal-code listings vary slightly (78000 vs 78340) across directories, but street number and colonia (Julián Carrillo) are stable. The article’s venue claim matches the official program.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — venue primary pages plus festival coverage.

### CLAIM 6: “500+ wines” / “more than 500 labels”

**CLAIM:** "more than 500 labels" / "500+ wine labels" / "Latin America's largest wine event by number of labels."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Official tourism copy and the festival site advertise “+500” / “más de 500 vinos.” Post-event Pulso again cites “más de 500 etiquetas.” The comparative “largest in Latin America by labels” is organizer marketing language repeated widely; it was not independently audited here, but the numeric “500+” claim itself is supported by primary materials.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [festivaldelvino.mx — “+500 Vinos de todo el Mundo”](https://festivaldelvino.mx/)
- **Primary Source:** [Secretaría de Turismo SLP — “más de 500 vinos de todo el mundo”](https://turismo.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/5/7/14-festival-internacional-del-vino/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Pulso SLP post-event — más de 500 etiquetas](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/status/14-festival-internacional-del-vino-san-luis/2054962)
- **Counter-evidence note:** [El Sol de San Luis headline — “más de mil etiquetas”](https://oem.com.mx/elsoldesanluis/local/festival-del-vino-reune-mas-de-mil-etiquetas-en-el-centro-de-las-artes-30270422) — secondary headline at odds with official “500+”; does not overturn the official count used in the article.

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article’s “500+” figure tracks the official program. A minority press headline claiming “1,000+” is unsupported by the organizer’s own site and tourism listing; if anything, it would make “500+” conservative, not false.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — for the 500+ count against official sources; Medium for the “largest in Latin America” ranking claim (not scored separately).

### CLAIM 9: Friday sold out as of April 2026; Saturday selling 30% faster than 2025

**CLAIM:** "Friday is already sold out; Saturday is selling 30% faster than in 2025" (as of April 28, 2026).

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No independent government bulletin, organizer press release, or third-party ticket-platform snapshot was found that confirms Friday sell-out status on or about April 28, 2026, or a quantified +30% Saturday sales rate versus 2025. Searches for those sales metrics only surface the San Luis Way article itself (and its Spanish twin).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** None located outside the article under review
- **Corroborating Source:** Not found — web searches for “viernes agotado,” “30%,” and ticket-velocity language returned unrelated wine events or only this post
- **Related context:** [festivaldelvino.mx](https://festivaldelvino.mx/) historically showed urgency language (“ÚLTIMOS BOLETOS”) for Silver during the sales window, but that is not a dated sell-out confirmation for Friday specifically

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Sell-out and year-over-year sales-velocity claims are checkable in principle but require organizer inventory data that was not published. Absence of contradiction is not confirmation. This is the only claim in the focus set that cannot be traced to a primary record.

**CONFIDENCE:** Low — no independent evidence either way.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Core program facts (edition, dates, venue, hours, prices, 500+ wines, 159 wineries, tasting/dinner counts, organizer) track official and local primary reporting. The April 28 guide accurately described the May 29–30, 2026 event that later took place. Soft marketing language (“largest in Latin America”) and projected economic impact (~17M) sit outside the hardest counts but were not scored as false.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Tone is promotional and urgency-driven (sell-out, “buy this week”), which is expected for a ticket guide. The unverifiable sales-velocity claim is the clearest place where persuasion outran sourcing.

### SOURCING QUALITY
The article’s cited stack (festivaldelvino.mx, Turismo SLP, CANIRAC, local press) is the right stack. Where those sources speak, the post matches them. Where they are silent (Friday sell-out / +30% sales), the post asserts without a public primary.
