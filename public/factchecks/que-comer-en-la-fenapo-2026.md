## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: FENAPO 2026 dates and Recinto Ferial venue (not Centro de Convenciones)

**CLAIM:** "The Feria Nacional Potosina runs August 7–30, 2026 at the Recinto Ferial (Francisco Martínez de la Vega 255, Col. Tepeyac)."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Multiple contemporaneous reports and the official FENAPO portal place the 2026 fair at the Recinto Ferial / FENAPO grounds, August 7–30. Post-event coverage confirms it opened Friday August 7 and closed Sunday August 30. That venue is distinct from the city’s Centro de Convenciones / Centro de Negocios Potosí (Boulevard Antonio Rocha Cordero 125, Desarrollo del Pedregal). The article correctly names the feria grounds, not the convention center.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — official contact address](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/) — Francisco Martínez de la Vega 255, Colonia Tepeyac, C.P. 78384
- **Corroborating Source:** [Telediario — Fenapo 2026: cuándo termina](https://www.telediario.mx/comunidad/fenapo-en-san-luis-potosi-2026-cuando-termina) — Aug 7–30, 2026; same avenue address
- **Corroborating Source:** [turismo.slp.gob.mx — CNPOTOSI / Centro de Convenciones](https://turismo.slp.gob.mx/CNPOTOSI/) — separate venue at Blvd. Antonio Rocha Cordero 125

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
As of September 21, 2026 the fair is over, but the stated dates and seat of the event match what actually happened. No credible source moved the 2026 edition to the Centro de Convenciones.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — official portal plus mid/post-fair reporting agree.

### CLAIM 3: Free entry to the fairgrounds (and thus the food area); free parking, rides, and Foro concerts

**CLAIM:** "Entry is free, and so are the parking, the mechanical rides and every concert at the Foro de las Estrellas." FAQ: free fairgrounds entry in 2026.

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
State and local coverage of the 2026 gratuidad model confirms free general admission, parking, mechanical rides, and Foro concerts. The Muestra Gastronómica and typical-food zones sit inside the paid-entry-free recinto, so access to the food area did not require a separate admission ticket. Food and drinks themselves are purchased (with at least one promotional free-food day announced during the fair), which the article does not confuse with free entry.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Potosinoticias — Fenapo 2026 entrada y atracciones gratuitas (Jun 18, 2026)](https://potosinoticias.com/2026/06/18/fenapo-2026-iniciara-en-agosto-con-entrada-y-atracciones-gratuitas/) — free entry, parking, rides, Foro
- **Corroborating Source:** [Líder Empresarial — FENAPO 2026 (Aug 6, 2026)](https://www.liderempresarial.com/fenapo-2026-buscara-romper-record-con-9-millones-de-visitantes/) — free entry, parking, rides, Foro; Muestra Gastronómica retained
- **Corroborating Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — avoid Foro ticket fraud (May 29, 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/2/fenapo-2026-llama-a-evitar-fraudes-ante-cartelera-sin-l%C3%ADmites-y-precedentes/) — Foro free; no ticket/QR/reservation

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
No source indicates a separate cover charge for the gastronomic pavilion beyond entering the free fairgrounds. Dish prices are paid to stands; the article’s free-entry claim is about admission, not free food.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — consistent official and press confirmation through the fair period.

### CLAIM 4: Only the Palenque charges; tickets via Fast Ticket; Foro seats are never sold

**CLAIM:** "Only the Palenque charges admission, with tickets sold exclusively through Fast Ticket — state authorities have warned that Foro seats are never sold, so treat anyone offering them as a scam."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Official FENAPO notices name SLP Fast Ticket (slpfastticket.com) and the Palenque box office as the authorized channels, warn against pirate pages and resellers, and state Foro presentations need no ticket, QR, or reservation and cannot be reserved or sold. That matches the article’s scam warning.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — evita fraudes / Foro gratuito](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/2/fenapo-2026-llama-a-evitar-fraudes-ante-cartelera-sin-l%C3%ADmites-y-precedentes/) — Foro free; official boletera channels listed
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — boletos Palenque / Fast Ticket (Jun 5, 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/5/gobierno-estatal-mantiene-disponibles-boletos-para-el-palenque-fenapo-2026/) — buy only via official boletera / box office
- **Corroborating Source:** [stps.slp.gob.mx — venta en slpfastticket.com](https://stps.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/4/fenapo-2026/) — online sales at https://slpfastticket.com

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Paid Terrazas VIP products are a separate paid product from free Foro general admission; the article’s claim that Foro seats are never sold tracks the Patronato’s wording. Palenque exclusivity via Fast Ticket (plus official box office) is correct.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — primary Patronato notices.

### CLAIM 5: Traditional gastronomic pavilion with cooks from the four state regions; food pavilions confirmed for 2026

**CLAIM:** "The heart of feria eating is the traditional gastronomic pavilion, where cooks from the state’s four regions — Huasteca, Zona Media, Altiplano and Centro — set up for the month… the state has confirmed food pavilions and a typical-food zone for 2026."

The four-region framing is the standard FENAPO muestra model (documented for 2025 and previewed for 2026). During FENAPO 2026 the Muestra Gastronómica operated with 36 kitchens and featured potosino dishes including enchiladas potosinas; Patronato previews also promised restaurants plus the traditional culinary muestra representing the four regions.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Líder Empresarial — restaurantes y muestra; cuatro regiones (Aug 6, 2026)](https://www.liderempresarial.com/fenapo-2026-buscara-romper-record-con-9-millones-de-visitantes/)

- **Corroborating Source:** [Potosinoticias — cuatro regiones / muestra 2025 (Jul 23, 2025)](https://potosinoticias.com/2025/07/23/cocina-de-las-cuatro-regiones-brillara-en-la-muestra-gastronomica-de-fenapo-2025/) — Huasteca, Altiplano, Zona Media, Centro model

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article’s forward-looking August 1 confirmation was borne out mid-fair. Post-event coverage shows a broader national guest slate as well; that expands, rather than contradicts, the four-region potosino core.

### CLAIM 7: Enchiladas potosinas — chile-kneaded masa, born in Soledad in 1919

**CLAIM:** "Enchiladas potosinas — the chile-kneaded masa icon, born in Soledad in 1919… invented in Soledad de Graciano Sánchez in 1919."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Local historiography and municipal/state commemorations attribute the dish to Cristina Jalomo of Soledad de Graciano Sánchez around 1919, with chile worked into the masa (not only as an outer sauce). The 2019 centennial in Soledad explicitly used that origin year. Mid-fair 2026 coverage also listed enchiladas potosinas among Muestra offerings.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Municipio de Soledad — FENAE 2019 / centenario](http://www.municipiosoledad.gob.mx/node/525) — Cristina Jalomo; ~100 years / 1919 framing
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — origen en Soledad hace 101 años (2020)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/municipios/23-10-2020/enchiladas-potosinas-el-platillo-originario-de-soledad-que-se-creo-por/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Wikipedia — Enchilada potosina](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enchilada_potosina) — chile in masa; Soledad; ~1919 / centennial citation

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Exact creation day is oral-history tradition rather than a notarized primary document, but the article’s 1919 / Soledad / chile-in-masa package matches the version recognized in local official commemorations.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — municipal and long-running local press consensus.

### CLAIM 8: Zacahuil — Huasteca giant tamal, meter-long, wood-fired overnight, sold by the slice; bocoles as Huasteca corn-and-lard rounds

**CLAIM:** "Zacahuil — the Huasteca’s giant tamal: meter-long, wood-fired overnight, sold by the slice… Bocoles — crisp-outside, tender-inside corn-and-lard rounds split and stuffed with queso, chicharrón or egg. The Huasteca’s answer to breakfast."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Culinary references (Larousse, UNAM Global, regional press) describe zacahuil as a Huasteca giant tamal often ~1–2 m, slow-cooked in a wood oven or earth oven for many hours / overnight, and sold by the portion. Bocoles are documented Huasteca antojitos of masa mixed with lard, cooked on a comal, split and stuffed — including cheese, chicharrón, or egg fillings.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Larousse Cocina — Zacahuil](https://laroussecocina.mx/palabra/zacahuil/) — Huasteca giant tamal; wood oven / texcal; sold by portions; overnight bake example
- **Corroborating Source:** [UNAM Global — zacahuil Huasteca](https://unamglobal.unam.mx/global_revista/zacahuil-tamal-huasteca-gastronomia-mexicana/) — meter-scale size; Huasteca origin
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — bocoles huastecos](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/bocoles-el-pequeno-antojito-huasteco-que-guarda-el-sabor-de-san-luis-potosi/) — masa, manteca, fillings including queso/chicharrón
- **Corroborating Source:** [Wikipedia — Bocol](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocol) — Huasteca origin; lard in masa; stuffed

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
“Meter-long” is a fair summary of commonly cited lengths (about one to two meters). Overnight wood-fired cooking and by-the-slice service match standard descriptions. No article claim asserted a specific FENAPO stand price for either dish.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — encyclopedic and regional culinary sources align.

### CLAIM 9: Many traditional muestra stands are cash-only; ATMs on site with long weekend lines

**CLAIM:** "Bring cash: many traditional stands don’t take cards… The bigger stands and national chains usually do, but many traditional cooks at the gastronomic pavilion are cash-only. Bring pesos; there are ATMs on site but lines get long on weekends."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No official FENAPO 2026 payment policy or ATM inventory for the Muestra Gastronómica was found in Patronato releases. Cash preference among traditional feria cooks is plausible and widely advised anecdotally, but it is not documented as an official rule for 2026. ATM presence and weekend queue length likewise lack a primary public source dated to this edition.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** None located for 2026 payment methods or on-site ATM operations
- **Corroborating Source:** Fair coverage focuses on kitchens, capacity, and free-admission logistics, not card acceptance ([Quadratín SLP, Aug 26, 2026](https://sanluispotosi.quadratin.com.mx/san-luis-potosi/sabores-de-slp-y-de-mexico-toman-la-muestra-gastronomica-de-fenapo/))

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Treated as practical traveler advice rather than a falsified official fact. Without a publishable primary source, the verdict is UNVERIFIABLE rather than FALSE.

**CONFIDENCE:** Low — absence of official documentation, not affirmative contradiction.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Logistics and dish-origin claims are strong. The piece avoids inventing peso prices for enchiladas, zacahuil, or other stands — so there is no pre-event price sheet that later needed updating after August 30. Venue naming correctly points to the Recinto Ferial, not the Centro de Convenciones.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Promotional travel-guide tone (“Mexico’s biggest state fair,” “best value meals”) appears, but checkable food and venue facts are not stretched to match it. Municipality examples are hedged as “recent editions,” which proved accurate.

### SOURCING QUALITY
FENAPO/state portals, June–August 2026 press, municipal Soledad material, and standard culinary references (Larousse, UNAM) support the factual spine. Payment-method advice is the weak spot.
