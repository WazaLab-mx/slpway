## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Official dates still “por confirmar” in August 2026

**CLAIM:** "Fechas: Agosto 2026 (inauguración y clausura oficial por confirmar)" — and that FENAPO 2026 “se celebrará en agosto de 2026.”

**VERDICT:** 🔄 OUTDATED

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The August window was correct, and the fair did run in August 2026. Official and press sources later fixed the calendar as 7–30 August 2026 (24 days), with inauguration coverage on 7 August and closure reporting through 30 August. By verification day (21 September 2026) the fair is over; leaving inauguration/closure “por confirmar” is superseded official information, not a live planning fact.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — A 50 días de la FENAPO 2026 (18 Jun 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/7/7/a-50-d%C3%ADas-de-la-fenapo-2026-una-fiesta-sin-l%C3%ADmites-para-todo-m%C3%A9xico/) — “Del 7 al 30 de agosto”
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — Arranca la Fenapo 2026](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/estado/arranca-la-fenapo-2026-asi-se-vivio-el-primer-dia-de-la-maxima-fiesta-de-verano-de-san-luis/) — opened 7 Aug, through 30 Aug
- **Corroborating Source:** [Potosinoticias — cierre con más de 10 millones de visitantes (31 Aug 2026)](https://potosinoticias.com/2026/08/31/fenapo-2026-supera-los-10-millones-de-visitantes-y-concluye-con-saldo-blanco/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
As an April 18, 2026 planning snapshot, “August, dates TBD” was a fair hedge. Against the program that actually ran, readers now need 7–30 August 2026. The article’s own later note that the cartel was published 27 May does not update the glance-box date line.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — multiple government and post-event sources converge on 7–30 August.

### CLAIM 2: Venue is Recinto Ferial at Av. Francisco Martínez de la Vega No. 255

**CLAIM:** "se celebrará … en el Recinto Ferial sobre Av. Fco. Martínez de la Vega" and "El Recinto Ferial está en Av. Fco. Martínez de la Vega No. 255."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The official Gobierno del Estado / FENAPO listing and every 2026 press kit use Francisco Martínez de la Vega N° 255, Colonia Tepeyac, C.P. 78384, San Luis Potosí. The fair did take place at that recinto.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [slp.gob.mx — Dependencia FENAPO](https://slp.gob.mx/dependencias/detalles/?dep=FENAPO) — Francisco Martínez de la Vega 255, Colonia Tepeyac, 78384
- **Corroborating Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx contact block (50-días notice)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/7/7/a-50-d%C3%ADas-de-la-fenapo-2026-una-fiesta-sin-l%C3%ADmites-para-todo-m%C3%A9xico/) — same street number and colonia

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Street number, avenue name, and recinto identity all match Tier-1/Tier-2 government pages. No competing official venue was announced for 2026.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — direct match to the state’s own address listing.

### CLAIM 3: Free entry, rides, parking, and Teatro del Pueblo; only Palenque is paid

**CLAIM:** "Para 2026 el Gobernador anunció que la entrada a la feria, los juegos mecánicos, el estacionamiento y los conciertos del Teatro del Pueblo serán gratuitos — solo el Palenque mantiene boletaje separado."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
State communications before and during the fair repeated permanent gratuidad for access, parking, mechanical rides, and Foro / Teatro del Pueblo shows. Palenque required paid tickets via the official boletera. Optional paid Terrazas at the free stage existed but do not overturn the article’s core free-vs-paid split.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Gobierno del Estado SLP — gratuidad de la FENAPO (Jan 2026)](https://slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/1/12/san-luis-potos%C3%AD-es-referente-nacional-con-la-gratuidad-de-la-fenapo/) — free parking, access, shows, and rides
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — 50 días (Jun 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/7/7/a-50-d%C3%ADas-de-la-fenapo-2026-una-fiesta-sin-l%C3%ADmites-para-todo-m%C3%A9xico/) — entrada, estacionamiento, juegos, Foro gratuitos
- **Corroborating Source:** [Infobae — conciertos gratuitos Foro de las Estrellas (27 May 2026)](https://www.infobae.com/mexico/2026/05/27/fenapo-2026-lista-oficial-de-conciertos-gratuitos-en-el-teatro-del-pueblo/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Post-event reporting still describes free Teatro del Pueblo / Foro access and paid Palenque. The article’s framing matches what the program delivered. A minor completeness gap is optional Terrazas seating, which was paid but not required for general Foro attendance.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — repeated on official portals and independent outlets.

### CLAIM 4: Buy Palenque tickets at fenapo.mx and eticket.mx

**CLAIM:** "Palenque: Boleto independiente — fenapo.mx y eticket.mx" and "Bookmarkea fenapo.mx y eticket.mx" / "eticket.mx es el canal oficial."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
When Palenque sales opened in early June 2026, the Gobierno / Patronato named https://slpfastticket.com as the online platform, plus the Palenque box office (10:00–18:00), with a four-ticket limit and warnings about pirate sites. Neither fenapo.mx nor eticket.mx was the authorized 2026 seller.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — Arranca venta de boletos Palenque (2 Jun 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/3/arranca-venta-de-boletos-para-el-palenque-fenapo-2026/) — plataforma oficial https://slpfastticket.com; taquilla 10:00–18:00; límite de cuatro
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — boletos disponibles / páginas piratas (4 Jun 2026)](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/5/gobierno-estatal-mantiene-disponibles-boletos-para-el-palenque-fenapo-2026/) — only official boletera channels; SLP Fast Ticket socials named
- **Corroborating Source:** [STPS SLP — FENAPO 2026 ticket notice (4 Jun 2026)](https://stps.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/4/fenapo-2026/) — venta en línea vía slpfastticket.com

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
This is the article’s most consequential error for readers who still treat it as a how-to: sending buyers to eticket.mx / fenapo.mx contradicts the state’s sole named online channel and the anti-fraud messaging that followed. fenapo.slp.gob.mx is the informational portal, not the checkout.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — contradicted by the Patronato’s own sales announcements.

### CLAIM 5: August hotel occupancy tops 90%; prices rise 50–100% from June

**CLAIM:** "August occupancy tops 90%, and prices rise 50–100% from June" / "La ocupación hotelera durante FENAPO supera el 90%."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Peak FENAPO nights did produce official claims of hotel occupancy above 90% (notably around the Katy Perry weekend). Broader August and pre-fair vacation figures were much lower (often ~50–62% reservations / ~60% occupancy averages), and other reports put the Perry-weekend figure near 80%. The blanket “August tops 90%” and the specific 50–100% dynamic-price jump from June lack a published sector-wide primary dataset.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Corroborating Source:** [Metropoli San Luis — Gallardo: ocupación hotelera superior al 90% (26 Aug 2026)](https://metropolisanluis.com/2026/08/ricardo-gallardo-reporta-320-mil-asistentes-en-la-fenapo-y-ocupacion-hotelera-superior-al-90/)
- **Counter-Evidence:** [La Voz de San Luis — ocupación hotelera del 80% tras Katy Perry (26 Aug 2026)](https://lavozdesanluis.com.mx/cultura/2026/08/26/noche-historica-en-fenapo-2026-con-katy-perry/)
- **Counter-Evidence:** [El Universal San Luis — 62% reservaciones previo a Katy Perry](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/economia-y-negocios/hoteles-de-slp-alcanzan-62-por-ciento-de-reservaciones-previo-al-concierto-de-katy-perry/); [Potosinoticias — ~60% ocupación arranque vacaciones (6 Aug 2026)](https://potosinoticias.com/2026/08/06/video-hoteles-registran-60-de-ocupacion-durante-el-arranque-de-las-vacaciones/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Advice to book early is directionally sound. Treating >90% occupancy as the continuous August baseline, and asserting a precise 50–100% price spike from June without citing AMHM/SECTUR tables, overstates what the public record supports.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — peak-night claims exist; continuous-August and price-percent claims do not harden.

### CLAIM 6: Public transit routes 23, 32 and 52; fare $12 MXN

**CLAIM:** "Transporte público: Rutas 23, 32 y 52 con transbordo. Funciona hasta las 10 PM aprox. $12 MXN por viaje."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Local 2026 coverage of how to reach the recinto lists conventional routes such as 01, 08, 09, 17, 21 and 28 — not 23, 32 and 52. The cash urban fare reported ahead of the fair was $13.50 MXN, not $12. During FENAPO the SCT also ran free special shuttles and free Línea 3 RedMetro options the article omits.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — rutas de camión a la Fenapo 2026 (4 Aug 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/fenapo-2026-estas-rutas-de-camion-son-las-que-te-llevan-hasta-la-maxima-fiesta-del-verano/) — rutas 01, 08, 09, 17, 21, 28; tarifa $13.50
- **Corroborating Source:** [Moovit — stop Francisco Martínez De La Vega 255](https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Francisco_Mart%C3%ADnez_De_La_Vega_255-San_Luis_Potos%C3%AD-stop_17907919-3742) — nearby lines include Ruta 07, 08, 21
- **Corroborating Source:** [Pulso SLP — cuatro rutas gratuitas SCT (7 Aug 2026)](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/slp/cuatro-rutas-gratuitas-llevaran-visitantes-a-la-fenapo-2026/2074068)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Wrong route numbers plus an outdated fare make this actionable logistics claim unreliable. The ~10 PM end of regular service is roughly consistent with reported last buses, but that does not salvage the identifiers or price.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — contemporaneous local transport reporting contradicts the named routes and $12 fare.

### CLAIM 7: Recinto is 8 km from Centro Histórico; Uber/DiDi $80–$150 MXN

**CLAIM:** "El Recinto Ferial está en Av. Fco. Martínez de la Vega No. 255, 8 km del Centro Histórico" and "Precio desde Centro: $80–$150 MXN."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Address is correct. Distance estimates in travel and prior-fair guides more often cite about 10–15 km or emphasize time-under-traffic rather than a fixed 8 km. The Uber/DiDi $80–$150 Centro range is a plausible planning band echoed in other local guides, but it is not an official tariff and varies with surge (which the article itself notes).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [slp.gob.mx — FENAPO address](https://slp.gob.mx/dependencias/detalles/?dep=FENAPO)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Mejor — cómo llegar FENAPO (2025 guide still used for distance framing)](https://lamejor.com.mx/news/2025/7/17/como-llegar-desde-san-luis-potosi-la-fenapo-2025-en-transporte-publico-19576.html) — ~10–15 km; Uber/taxi band $80–$150
- **Corroborating Source:** [Guía Turista México — cómo llegar desde el Centro](https://www.guiaturista.com.mx/san-luis-potosi/como-llegar-fenapo-centro-san-luis-potosi-perderte-n6639) — stresses time/traffic over a single kilometer figure

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Treat 8 km as an approximate road distance that may understate common published ranges; treat rideshare prices as illustrative, not guaranteed. Core advice to prefer rideshare over parking on big nights remains sensible.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — address solid; km and fare are soft estimates.

### CLAIM 8: FENAPO 2026 is “edición 77”

**CLAIM:** "La FENAPO 2026 — edición 77 de la Feria Nacional Potosina de México"

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Official 2026 notices consistently say “edición 2026,” not “edición 77.” History pages confirm the first Gran Feria Potosina in 1942 and COVID cancellations in 2020–2021, but no primary 2026 government release found for this investigation assigns ordinal 77. Simple year-count math from 1942 does not uniquely produce 77 without an unpublished numbering rule.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [fenapo.slp.gob.mx — Nuestra historia](https://fenapo.slp.gob.mx/nuestra_historia/) — origins in 1942; no 2026 ordinal “77”
- **Corroborating Source:** [Potosinoticias — primeras ediciones / 1942](https://potosinoticias.com/2023/08/07/una-mirada-al-pasado-asi-fueron-las-primeras-ediciones-y-sedes-de-la-feria-nacional-potosina/)
- **Absence check:** 2026 government inauguration/closure copy refers to “edición 2026,” not edición 77

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Without a Patronato or gobierno numbering statement for 2026, the “77” label cannot be confirmed or firmly falsified from public primary sources alone.

**CONFIDENCE:** Low — historical start year is solid; the ordinal is not.

### CLAIM 9: Palenque “gradas” budget $600–$1,200 MXN

**CLAIM:** "Día Palenque ~$1,500 MXN" with "Palenque (gradas): $600–$1,200" based on “tarifas típicas FENAPO 2024–2025 ajustadas por inflación 2026.”

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Published General (lowest zone) prices for Palenque 2026 clustered about $700–$1,200 MXN depending on the artist (e.g., several dates at $700; Grupo Firme General $1,200), so the upper bound and overall ballpark are useful. The $600 floor is below published General lists, and VIP/Dorado/Diamante zones ran far higher (into the thousands), so treating $600–$1,200 as the Palenque ticket story understates real choice and cost.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Mejor — precios por zona Palenque FENAPO 2026 (4 Jun 2026)](https://lamejor.com.mx/news/2026/6/4/feria-nacional-potosina-2026-precios-zonas-venta-de-boletos-para-los-conciertos-en-el-palenque-27222.html) — General from $700; Firme General $1,200; higher zones up to $5,200 Diamante
- **Primary Source:** [STPS / fenapo notices](https://stps.slp.gob.mx/noticias/2026/6/4/fenapo-2026/) — prices plus service charge; buy on Fast Ticket
- **Corroborating Source:** [Global Media León — General $700–$1,300 framing](https://leon.globalmedia.mx/articles/fenapo_2026_ya_vendio_80_de_sus_espacios_comerciales)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
As a pre-sale inflation guess from prior years, the range nearly anticipated General pricing, but $600 is too low versus the 2026 list and “gradas” alone ignores the official zone ladder and service fees.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — General list prices are well reported; the article’s floor and labeling are soft.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Structural facts that stayed true through the actual fair — location, free-access model, August timing — hold up. Operational how-to details invented or carried from older seasons (ticket platforms, bus route numbers, exact fare, continuous 90% occupancy) fail or only partly survive contact with 2026 primary sources.

### BIAS INDICATORS
The piece is a preparation/marketing guide written in April, optimistic about booking urgency and free offerings. It does not appear to push a political narrative, but it over-promises certainty on logistics channels that later changed (or were never official).

### SOURCING QUALITY
The article cites fenapo.slp.gob.mx, feriafenapo.com, Noticieros SLP, and Frontal Noticias, yet still named eticket.mx / fenapo.mx for paid tickets — contradicting the Patronato’s June Fast Ticket announcements that actually governed sales.
