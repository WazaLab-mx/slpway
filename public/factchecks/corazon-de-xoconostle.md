## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Founded in 2014 with 10+ years of adventure tourism experience

**CLAIM:** "Founded in 2014, Corazón de Xoconostle is a leading adventure tourism company in San Luis Potosí with 10+ years of experience" / "Decade of Experience: Founded in 2014, 10+ years specialized in adventure tourism."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The operator’s website and LinkedIn company field both date the project to 2014, and as of September 2026 that is more than a decade from founding. The same primary sources state formal tour guiding began in 2016, so “10+ years specialized in adventure tourism” overstates the adventure-operator phase if read strictly from 2014. LinkedIn’s About text also says the project “nace en 2013,” creating a one-year inconsistency with the 2014 founding field.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Corazón de Xoconostle — Inicio / “10 Años” history](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [LinkedIn — Corazón de Xoconostle (Founded 2014; About text 2013/2016)](https://linkedin.com/company/corazon-de-xoconostle)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Founding-era hospitality in 2013–2014 and formal tourism operations from 2016 are both self-documented. By verification date, ~12 years from 2014 and ~10 years from 2016 make “10+ years” directionally fair, but packaging that decade as continuous specialized adventure tourism since founding is incomplete. No independent government founding record was located.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Consistent self-reporting across channels; no third-party corporate registry hit for the 2014 date.

### CLAIM 2: “Leading” adventure tourism company in San Luis Potosí

**CLAIM:** "Discover the leading adventure tourism company in San Luis Potosí" / "has grown… to become the leading adventure tourism company in San Luis Potosí."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No SECTUR ranking, market-share study, award list, or independent industry ranking was found that places Corazón de Xoconostle first among SLP adventure operators. The claim is promotional language repeated on San Luis Way; the company’s own site markets itself as an experienced operator without an equivalent “número uno” metric.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [San Luis Way article (claim origin)](https://www.sanluisway.com/blog/corazon-de-xoconostle)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Yumping — operator profile (active multi-adventure listing, no statewide rank)](https://www.yumping.com/en/multiadventure/corazon-de-xoconostle--e19748396)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Existence as a real multi-activity operator is not in doubt; “leading” is not a checkable fact without a defined metric. Absence of contradiction is not confirmation.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Searches for rankings and awards returned no supporting primary evidence.

### CLAIM 3: Office address Independencia 1585, Barrio de San Miguelito

**CLAIM:** "Address: Independencia 1585, Barrio de San Miguelito" and "Visit us at our office in the historic center of San Luis Potosí."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The operator’s website footer, LinkedIn primary location, and Yumping listing all give Independencia 1585, Barrio de San Miguelito, C.P. 78339. Barrio de San Miguelito is a founding barrio inside the city’s historic/monument zone, so “historic center” is geographically fair. An associated hostel historically used 5 de Mayo 1040 in the same barrio; that does not contradict the tour-office address.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Corazón de Xoconostle — contact block](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [LinkedIn — Independencia #1585](https://linkedin.com/company/corazon-de-xoconostle)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Yumping — Independencia 1585 listing](https://www.yumping.com/en/multiadventure/corazon-de-xoconostle--e19748396)
- **Corroborating Source:** [INAH / Astrolabio — Independencia in San Miguelito historic-monument perimeter](https://www.astrolabio.com.mx/inah-autoriza-rehabilitacion-del-barrio-de-san-miguelito-y-ordena-conservar-el-adoquin/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Street, colonia, and postal code match across independent commercial listings. Historic-center framing is supported by barrio heritage coverage and INAH perimeter references to Independencia within San Miguelito.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent listings agree on the same street address.

### CLAIM 5: Huasteca popular activities — Tamul, rappelling, water activities, cenote exploration

**CLAIM:** "Huasteca Potosina… Popular Activities: Tamul Waterfall; Rappelling and rappel; Water activities; Cenote exploration."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Tamul day trips, rappel options (Tamul side, Minas Viejas, Sótano del Cepillo), rafting, and swimming/waterfall visits are clearly sold. “Cenote exploration” is not supported: Huasteca product copy emphasizes waterfalls, rivers, pangas, cuevas/sótanos, and swimming holes—not cenotes. Yucatán-style cenote tourism is a different regional product.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Cascada de Tamul packages (panga, rappel add-ons, Cueva El Agua)](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/cascada-de-tamul.html)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Huasteca catalog — Tamul, rafting, Puente de Dios, Sótano del Cepillo](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/huasteca.html)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Three of four listed activity types check out. Labeling subterranean or cave features as “cenotes” would be a stretch not used on the operator’s own Huasteca pages. Published day-trip prices for core Huasteca routes cluster around $2,850 MXN (with some pages still showing older Tamul figures such as $1,550), though the San Luis Way article itself states no prices.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Gap is limited to the cenote label; other activities are explicit on primary pages.

### CLAIM 6: NOM-09-TUR-2002, NOM-011-TUR-2017, and Ministry of Tourism registration

**CLAIM:** "Guides certified NOM-09-TUR-2002; NOM-011-TUR-2017 Certification; Registration with Ministry of Tourism; Civil liability insurance."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The company repeatedly markets NOM-09-aligned guides and NOM-011 specialization (website packages, LinkedIn specialties). Mexican Prestadores de Servicios Turísticos must enroll in SECTUR’s Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT), but no public RNT certificate number or SECTUR listing for “Corazón de Xoconostle” / Isaac Pérez Vázquez was retrieved in this investigation. Insurance is advertised as trip/outdoor accident cover on itineraries; a civil-liability policy document was not located.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Operator — “Guías Certificados… NOM-09-TUR-2002” and history citing NOM-011](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [LinkedIn specialties — NOM-09, NOM-011, Rescue3](https://linkedin.com/company/corazon-de-xoconostle)
- **Corroborating Source:** [SECTUR Ventanilla de Turismo — RNT obligation overview](https://www.ventanillaturismo.gob.mx/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Self-assertion is consistent and package inclusions name NOM-09 guides, which raises credibility but does not replace a public credential check. Without an RNT hit or scanned certificates, Ministry registration and NOM compliance remain unconfirmed rather than disproven.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Strong self-documentation; missing Tier-1 registry confirmation.

### CLAIM 7: Day tours need 24 hours’ notice; expeditions need 1 week

**CLAIM:** "Day tours need 24 hours’ notice, expeditions 1 week" / "Day Tours: Book 24 hours in advance; Expeditions: Book 1 week in advance."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
These lead times appear on the San Luis Way post but were not found as a published booking policy on corazondexoconostle.com product or home pages reviewed. The operator asks travelers to reserve via WhatsApp/email and lists dated 2026 group departures with limited capacity, which implies advance booking without specifying 24 hours vs. one week.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [San Luis Way article (sole clear statement of 24h / 1 week)](https://www.sanluisway.com/blog/corazon-de-xoconostle)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Operator home — Calendario 2026 / contact booking](https://www.corazondexoconostle.com/en/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The rule may be operational practice shared with San Luis Way editors, but it cannot be verified from public primary materials. No contradictory shorter/longer official policy was found either.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Absence on the operator site is the limiting factor.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Identity facts (what the business is, where it sits, which destinations it sells) hold up. Soft marketing (“leading”), credential badges, and booking-window numbers are the weak spots. Huasteca activity lists are mostly right but import a “cenote” label that the operator does not use.

### BIAS INDICATORS
The piece reads as a partner/promotional guide written in first-person “our” voice, with an anonymous glowing testimonial. That favors superlatives over measurable comparisons and elevates self-reported certifications without linking to SECTUR proofs.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Strong primary operator web presence and commercial listings (Yumping, LinkedIn). Weak independent press coverage and no retrieved RNT certificate. The article states no tour prices; current operator day-trip prices for flagship Huasteca/Real de Catorce routes commonly publish from about $2,850 MXN, with some page-level inconsistencies (e.g., older Tamul $1,550 figures still visible on alternate URLs).
