## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Doña Juanita since 1959 at Jardín de Tequis

**CLAIM:** "Tacos Rojos de Doña Juanita at Jardín de Tequisquiapan (since 1959), on the corner of Mariano Arista and Mariano Ávila."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Local press repeatedly places Juana Paredes Torres (“Doña Juanita”) at the Mariano Arista / Mariano Ávila corner of Jardín de Tequis and ties the stand’s tacos-rojos tradition to 1959. Municipal and gubernatorial recognitions for decades of service align with that founding window. The business remains a family operation after her retirement.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Quadratín SLP — Tacos rojos Juanita (2019)](https://sanluispotosi.quadratin.com.mx/principal/tacos-rojos-juanita-tradicion-en-slp/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — Taquitos rojos, tradición del Barrio de Tequis (2018)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/especiales/26-08-2018/taquitos-rojos-tradicion-del-barrio-de-tequis/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — 63 años de tacos rojos de Tequis](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/los-emblematicos-tacos-rojos-de-tequis-63-anos-deleitando-el-paladar-de-los/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Address, neighborhood, signature style (tacos rojos), and “since 1959” all match independent local reporting. Origin stories also mention a predecessor named Camila/Camilita, which supports the article’s Camila / “tacos Camila” framing without contradicting Doña Juanita’s 1959 start.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent local outlets agree on location and year.

### CLAIM 2: Doña Juanita order price $60 MXN for six (2022)

**CLAIM:** "Reference price: $60 MXN for the order of six (2022) — expect a bit more today."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
A January 2022 La Orquesta interview quotes Doña Juanita saying an order of six dressed tacos cost 60 pesos. The article correctly dates the figure and warns that prices have moved, so it is not presented as a current menu price.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Orquesta — Los tacos rojos de Tequis: 62 años de tradición (5 Jan 2022)](https://laorquesta.mx/los-tacos-rojos-de-tequis-62-anos-de-tradicion/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Orquesta mirror — same interview](https://laorquesta.mx/los-tacos-rojos-de-tequis-62-anos-de-tradicion-2/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The claim is accurate as a 2022 press-verified reference. No 2026 street price was confirmed in this pass; the article’s “expect a bit more today” caveat prevents treating $60 as current.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Direct owner quote in dated local coverage.

### CLAIM 3: Los Agachados since 1960 by the Alameda / Carmen, evenings only

**CLAIM:** "Los Agachados, Alameda Juan Sarabia by Templo del Carmen · tacos rojos · since 1960. Evenings only, roughly 5 pm to midnight."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
El Universal (2022) reports the stand began in 1960 with Natalia González and Diega Hernández, was later relocated to Alameda Juan Sarabia facing Iglesia del Carmen, and operates roughly 17:00 to midnight (or until food runs out). The “agachados” name from low benches is also confirmed in that interview.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal SLP — Tacos rojos de Los Agachados (14 Sep 2022)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/tacos-rojos-de-los-agachados-una-tradicion-de-la-gastronomia-potosina-para-deleitar/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Uber Eats — Los Agachados listing](https://www.ubereats.com/mx/store/los-agachados/GSVqFGYxRb2EiQh5Bl1JjA)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Founding year, current Alameda / Carmen placement, evening service window, and signature tacos rojos (plus pambazos/gorditas) all check out. Early hours were once longer (15:00–03:00); today’s published pattern matches the article’s “evenings only” summary.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Owner interview plus active delivery listing.

### CLAIM 4: Tacos Joven “since 1949” at exactly one taquería

**CLAIM:** "The taco Joven… served at exactly one taquería since 1949."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The mustard-fried taco Joven is a real San Luis original still sold at the family’s Damián Carmona location, and a September 2024 El Universal profile uses 1949 as the founding year. An earlier August 2018 El Universal family interview instead says Esteban Vázquez started selling in 1937. The family also historically ran more than one point of sale (bus stand; Pedro Moreno branch), though the 2024 report says only the Damián Carmona local remains.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal SLP — Tacos Joven desde 1949 (15 Sep 2024)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/tacos-joven-un-legado-potosino-que-sigue-cautivando-paladares-desde-1949/)
- **Counter-Evidence:** [El Universal SLP — Súbale hay comida: Tacos joven (11 Aug 2018)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/economia-y-negocios/subale-hay-comida-tacos-joven/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Core claim that the dish is a one-recipe family specialty with deep mid-20th-century roots is sound. “Since 1949” is the newer press consensus but conflicts with a prior family-sourced 1937 date. “Exactly one taquería since 1949” overstates continuity of exclusivity given prior branches and other similarly named listings in business directories.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Existence and uniqueness of the recipe are strong; the absolute year and “only ever one” framing are not.

### CLAIM 5: Tacos Joven address, style, 2024 price, and hours

**CLAIM:** "Tacos Joven, Damián Carmona 1025, Barrio de Santiago — beef tacos fried with mustard and drowned in chilaca-tomato rajas salsa, pickled pig trotters on the side. Around $80 MXN for the order of four (2024). Open 6–11 pm daily."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The 2024 El Universal interview with current operator Luis Fernando Meléndez confirms Damián Carmona 1025, the mustard + rajas preparation, patitas curtidas, $80 for four tacos, and a longstanding 18:00–23:00 service window. Directory listings place the stand on that avenue in Barrio de Santiago.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal SLP — Tacos Joven (15 Sep 2024)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/tacos-joven-un-legado-potosino-que-sigue-cautivando-paladares-desde-1949/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Waze — Tacos joven, Av. Damián Carmona](https://www.waze.com/live-map/directions/tacos-joven-av.-damian-carmona-san-luis-potosi?to=place.w.169738462.1697515688.9644007)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Restaurant Guru — Tacos Joven Barrio de Santiago](https://es.restaurantguru.com/Tacos-Santiago-San-Luis-Potosi)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Address, signature style, 2024 reference price, and evening hours match the operator interview. Some aggregators list a slightly later open (19:00); the article’s “roughly 6–11 pm” tracks the owner’s stated schedule.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Owner-sourced details with location corroboration.

### CLAIM 6: La Fragua since 1987, Carranza 2380, pioneered pastor, started in Lomas

**CLAIM:** "La Fragua Steak Taco, Av. Carranza 2380 + branches · pastor & steak · since 1987. Widely credited with pioneering pastor in SLP. Started in Lomas in 1987; now a small local chain."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The brand’s official site states La Fragua was founded in 1987 by Armando López Zamarrón, lists Av. Venustiano Carranza #2380, claims it brought taco al pastor to the city first, and shows additional Lomas-area branches (Sierra Leona, Montes Verdes). The specific claim that the *first* 1987 opening was in Lomas is not confirmed by the official history, which only says a small shop on “one of the city’s most important avenues.”

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Fragua official site](http://lafraguamx.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Fragua historia PDF](http://www.lafraguasteaktaco.com.mx/historia.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Uber Eats — La Fragua Steak Taco 1987 (Carranza)](https://www.ubereats.com/mx/store/la-fragua-steak-taco-1987-carranza/InhitSLrRIeL8vwP5pG6dw)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Year, Carranza address, multi-branch status, and the company’s own “pioneered pastor in SLP” claim are supported. “Started in Lomas in 1987” remains unverified; Lomas locations exist today but may not be the original site. Absolute “first pastor in the city” is a self-claim without independent historical census of earlier trompos.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium-High — Strong on year/address/chain; soft on Lomas-as-origin and absolute pioneer status.

### CLAIM 7: El Camelluco at Mariano Otero 400, 30+ years, long hours

**CLAIM:** "El Camelluco, Mariano Otero 400, Tequisquiapan · pastor, machitos, alambres · 30+ years. Long hours (roughly 8:30 am–11:30 pm)."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Delivery and directory listings confirm Tacos El Camelluco at Mariano Otero 400 in Tequisquiapan with pastor, arrachera, chorizo, alambres, and related items. Published hours cluster around morning-to-late-evening (e.g., ~08:30–00:00/00:30 or 09:00–23:00). A firm “30+ years” founding date was not found; DENUE-style public records show a July 2010 registration, which would be ~16 years by 2026 unless the business predates formal registration.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Uber Eats — Tacos El Camelluco (Mariano Otero 400)](https://www.ubereats.com/mx/store/tacos-el-camelluco/kUYtrOX0W3m-tpb7fPyzEw)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Rappi — Tacos el Camelluco, Mariano Otero 400](https://www.rappi.com.mx/restaurantes/delivery/20626-tacos-el-camelluco)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Guiamexicano / DENUE-style listing — registered July 2010](https://guiamexican.com/directorio/3666773/tacos-el-camelluco)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Existence, address, neighborhood, and menu mix are solid. Hours are approximately right. “30+ years” is the weak link and should be treated as unverified marketing language unless a primary founding source appears.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Location and menu confirmed; tenure claim not.

### CLAIM 8: Los Dionisios Centro — ~6,000 guisado/canasta tacos a day

**CLAIM:** "Tacos Los Dionisios, Centro Histórico · breakfast guisado tacos… Local press puts their output around 6,000 tacos a day."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
El Universal’s October 2025 Centro Histórico food guide describes Los Dionisios as the popular “tacos de hielera” / canasta institution and states they serve about 6,000 tacos a day, with frijol, papa, huevo rojo, and chicharrón carnudo among the fillings—matching the article.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal SLP — Guía Centro Histórico (2 Oct 2025)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/guia-rapida-para-comer-rico-y-barato-en-el-centro-historico-de-san-luis-potosi/)
- **Corroborating Source:** Business directories listing “Tacos de Canasta Dionicios/Dionisios” in San Luis Potosí

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article accurately attributes the 6,000 figure to local press rather than presenting it as audited data. Style (morning guisados from coolers/hieleras) and Centro association are consistent with that reporting. Independent volume audits were not available.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium-High — Press attribution is correct; the volume figure itself is journalistic, not official statistics.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Institutional names, neighborhoods, signature styles (rojos, Joven mustard/rajas, pastor chains, canasta/guisados), and press-dated prices hold up well. Soft spots cluster on founding-year folklore and “only / first / 30+ years” superlatives.

### BIAS INDICATORS
The piece is a local dining guide, not neutral encyclopedia writing: it favors iconic stands and “where locals line up” framing. Subjective “best” language was out of scope; checkable institutional claims were generally careful and often self-hedged on prices.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Strong reliance on El Universal San Luis Potosí, Quadratín, La Orquesta, and official/business listings. Few government primary records for founding years; volume and “pioneer” claims rest on press or self-description.
