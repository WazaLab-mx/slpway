The protected-area facts (Sierra de San Miguelito decree, hectares and municipalities; Sierra de Álvarez status), the safety context and the guide-certification standard all match official sources. ## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Sierra de San Miguelito became a federal Flora and Fauna Protection Area in December 2021, covering 111,160 ha in four municipalities

**CLAIM:** "The Sierra de San Miguelito became a federal Flora and Fauna Protection Area in December 2021, covering 111,160 hectares across SLP, Mexquitic, Villa de Reyes and Villa de Arriaga."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
SEMARNAT's own presentation of the decree gives publication in the DOF on 13 December 2021 and an area of 111,160.44 ha as an Área de Protección de Flora y Fauna. The four municipalities are Mexquitic de Carmona, San Luis Potosí, Villa de Arriaga and Villa de Reyes. National media coverage of the decree matches this.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SEMARNAT, APFF Sierra de San Miguelito briefing (May 2024)](https://eventos.semarnat.gob.mx/files/uploads/rodolfo/2024/06/12/inf_san-miguelito_28052024.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [SEMARNAT press release, 14 Dec 2021](https://www.sinembargo.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/CPM-Semarnat-Sierra-San-Miguelito-14dic21.pdf); [Expansión Política, keys to the San Miguelito conflict](https://politica.expansion.mx/estados/2021/12/22/claves-conflicto-sierra-de-san-miguelito)

**CONFIDENCE:** High. The figures come from a federal primary source.

### CLAIM 2: The area's management programme was published in the DOF in June 2024

**CLAIM:** "Its management rules, published in the DOF in June 2024, ask hikers to stay on established trails, carry out all rubbish, keep dogs on a leash…"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
SEMARNAT's May 2024 briefing on the APFF accompanies the management-programme process. Trade press reports that the Programa de Manejo was published in the DOF on 28 June. The rules described (stay on trails, pack out rubbish, leash dogs, fires only where allowed) are standard APFF management rules.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SEMARNAT, APFF Sierra de San Miguelito briefing (May 2024)](https://eventos.semarnat.gob.mx/files/uploads/rodolfo/2024/06/12/inf_san-miguelito_28052024.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Imagen Agropecuaria, management programme published in DOF](https://imagenagropecuaria.com/etiqueta/aprovechamiento-sustentable/)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. The 28 June publication is confirmed, but the year was inferred from the SEMARNAT document's timing rather than read directly from the DOF.

### CLAIM 3: Parque Tangamanga I is a 420-hectare state park with free entry and early-morning hours

Several sources give the park's area as 420 hectares, opened in 1983. A tourism guide lists Monday hours of 5:00–11:00. Recent state coverage describes renovated facilities and extended lighting. No source contradicts free entry, which the post attributes to El Sol de San Luis (April 2026).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. The area and Monday hours are corroborated. The full weekly timetable could not be re-checked against a current Cecurt page.

### CLAIM 4: Joya Honda is about 1.4 km across and some 300 m deep, protected as a state natural monument

**CLAIM:** "A dry volcanic crater about 1.4 km across and some 300 m deep, protected as a state natural monument."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Popular sources give 1.4 km × 300 m. Academic work (Aranda-Gómez & Luhr; UASLP research record) measures the maar at about 1,200 × 930 m. The state signed the decree making it a Natural Monument ANP in April 2021, so the protection status is correct. The width now reads "roughly 1.2–1.4 km across".

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [UASLP research record, Joya Honda maar ~1200 × 930 m](https://orbis.uaslp.mx/vivo/display/d11452)
- **Corroborating Source:** [México Desconocido, ANP Monumento Natural Joya Honda](https://escapadas.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/area-natural-protegida-monumento-natural-joya-honda/); [Wikipedia, Ventura volcanic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_volcanic_field)

**CONFIDENCE:** High. The figures differ between popular and scientific sources. The range now covers both.

### CLAIM 6: The Sierra de Álvarez has similar CONANP rules

**CLAIM:** "The Sierra de Álvarez has similar CONANP rules."

**VERDICT:** 🟠 MISLEADING

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The Sierra de Álvarez is a federal APFF of 16,900 ha. However, a 2023 Chamber of Deputies point of agreement notes that it still had no management programme, and in 2026 the SLP Congress asked authorities to draft and publish one. So no specific rules like San Miguelito's 2024 programme are formally in force. The sentence now says the area is federally protected but its management programme is still unpublished, and advises following the same practices.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Gaceta Parlamentaria, Chamber of Deputies (Nov 2023): Sierra de Álvarez without management programme](https://gaceta.diputados.gob.mx/Gaceta/65/2023/nov/20231114-III-2.html)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Sol de San Luis, Congress asks for a Sierra de Álvarez plan](https://oem.com.mx/elsoldesanluis/local/congreso-del-estado-pide-plan-de-trabajo-para-proteger-sierra-de-alvarez-30231728); [La Orquesta, Sierra de Álvarez loses court protection (Feb 2026)](https://laorquesta.mx/sierra-de-alvarez-slp/)

**CONFIDENCE:** High. Two independent sources show the management programme is still pending.

### CLAIM 7: Cerro Potosí in Nuevo León is a different mountain of about 3,700 m

**CLAIM:** "Cerro Potosí in Nuevo León is a different mountain of about 3,700 m."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Cerro El Potosí in Galeana, Nuevo León is the highest peak of the Sierra Madre Oriental. Sources give 3,715–3,721 m, so "about 3,700 m" is accurate. It is clearly distinct from the ~2,650 m summit south of SLP city.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Wikipedia, Cerro Potosí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerro_Potos%C3%AD)
- **Corroborating Source:** [México Desconocido, Cerro del Potosí, Galeana](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/galeana-nuevo-leon-cerro-del-potosi.html)

**CONFIDENCE:** High.

### CLAIM 8: Cerro del Quemado is a sacred Wixárika site at about 2,940 m; the 3,800 m figure is wrong

**CLAIM:** "The Cerro del Quemado is a sacred ceremonial site for the Wixárika (Huichol) people in Wirikuta… at about 2,940 m by GPS… Some websites claim 3,800 m; GPS data says otherwise."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The Wixárika consider Cerro Quemado one of their most sacred sites in Wirikuta, where the sun first rose. Real de Catorce town sits at 2,728 m, and La Jornada places the summit at about 3,000 m, which is consistent with ~2,940 m by GPS. Tourism pages claiming 3,800 m are clearly inflated.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Jornada, Wirikuta and mining (summit ~3,000 m)](https://www.jornada.com.mx/2012/02/08/sociedad/042n2soc)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Wikipedia, Real de Catorce (2,728 m)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_de_Catorce); [Zona Turística, Cerro del Quemado](https://www.zonaturistica.com/que-hacer/san-luis-potosi/real-de-catorce/cerro-del-quemado)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. No survey-grade elevation was found, but all credible figures fall near 2,900–3,000 m.

### CLAIM 9: Real de Catorce is about 213 km / 3 h 30 min from SLP

**CLAIM:** "Drive from SLP: About 213 km / 3 h 30 min, plus the Ogarrio tunnel."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The road route runs on Highway 57 to Matehuala (~190 km), then via Cedral to the Ogarrio tunnel. Published distances are 239 km (Uno TV), ~250 km (Sygic) and 256 km (Hertz México), with about 3.5 hours of driving. The 213 km figure understates the trip by roughly 35–40 km. It now reads "About 250 km / 3 h 30 min".

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Hertz México, Real de Catorce guide (256 km, ~3.5 h)](https://www.hertzmexico.com/en/blog/travel/real-de-catorce-and-the-best-places-to-visit-in-town/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Uno TV, Real de Catorce (239 km from SLP)](https://www.unotv.com/turismo/pueblo-magico-de-real-de-catorce-san-luis-potosi-la-leyenda-viviente-de-un-pueblo-de-piedra/); [Sygic Travel, Real de Catorce](https://guides.travel.sygic.com/production/en/Real_de_Catorce)

**CONFIDENCE:** High. Three independent sources agree on roughly 240–256 km.

### CLAIM 10: SLP city sits at about 1,860 m and May averages a 28 °C high

**CLAIM:** "SLP city sits at about 1,860 m… April and May are hot (May averages a 28 °C high)."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The city's elevation of about 1,860 m is standard. Climate normals for SLP (WeatherSpark, Ponciano Arriaga airport; Population Review 1970–2000 normals) both put May's average high at about 29 °C, not 28 °C. Changed to 29 °C.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [WeatherSpark, SLP airport climate (May high 29 °C)](https://es.weatherspark.com/y/145778/Clima-promedio-en-Aeropuerto-Internacional-Ponciano-Arriaga-M%C3%A9xico-durante-todo-el-a%C3%B1o)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Population Review, San Luis Potosí climate](https://www.populationreview.org/climate/mexico/san-luis-potosi)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. These are modelled and station normals, not official SMN tables, but both sources agree.

El Sol de San Luis ran a 2026 story headlined that at least six hikers had got lost in the Potosí sierras so far that year. Earlier Sierra de Álvarez rescues (Quadratín, Código San Luis, Pulso) confirm the pattern of hikers getting lost rather than being attacked.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**

### CLAIM 13: Spot San Luis guides are certified under NOM-09-TUR-2002

**CLAIM:** "Spot San Luis runs community hikes… with certified guides (NOM-09-TUR-2002)."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
NOM-09-TUR-2002 is the current Mexican standard for specialised guides, including nature- and adventure-oriented guides. It is listed as current by the Secretaría de Economía. The standard is cited correctly. The operator's own certification was not independently audited.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Secretaría de Economía, NOM-09-TUR-2002](https://platiica.economia.gob.mx/normalizacion/nom-09-tur-2002/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [UNDP Mexico, diploma for nature guides under NOM-09-TUR-2002](https://www.undp.org/es/mexico/kuxatur/caja-de-herramientas/desarrollo-de-habilidades-para-el-turismo-sostenible/diplomado-para-la-formacion-de-guias-de-turistas-especializados-con-orientacion-hacia-la-naturaleza-nom-09-tur-2002)

**CONFIDENCE:** High for the standard. Medium for the operator's compliance.

### CLAIM 14: The Sendero del Capitán is a ~19 km route linking Cerro de San Pedro, Monte Caldera and Armadillo, tied to Captain Miguel Caldera

Tour listings describe the route from Cerro de San Pedro through Monte Caldera to Armadillo de los Infante, about 19 km, named for Captain Miguel Caldera, son of a Guachichil woman and a Castilian captain. A Wikiloc track confirms the trail is divided into named sections.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Civitatis, Sendero del Capitán hiking tour](https://www.civitatis.com/es/san-luis-potosi/ruta-pie-sendero-capitan/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Wikiloc, Sendero El Capitán, Armadillo section](https://no.wikiloc.com/hiking-trails/sendero-el-capitan-armadillo-jaguey-armadillo-57189293)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. The distance comes from a tour operator, but it matches the post's GPS range.
