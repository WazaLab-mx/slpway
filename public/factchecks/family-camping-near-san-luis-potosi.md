## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Media Luna entry is MXN 100 adults / MXN 50 children and seniors, tent camping MXN 150 per tent, day hours 8:00–17:00, cash only

**CLAIM:** "Tent MXN 150 plus entry (adults MXN 100, children and seniors MXN 50)… day hours 8:00–17:00… Cash only."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
El Universal San Luis (May 2025) lists MXN 100 adults and MXN 50 for children and seniors, MXN 150 per tent regardless of size, and hours of 8:00–17:00. Milenio (April 2025) gives the same adult and tent prices and hours. México Desconocido notes that cards are not accepted. The post's 2026 source (Plano Informativo) is consistent with these.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal San Luis, how much is entry to the Media Luna](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/cuanto-cuesta-la-entrada-a-la-laguna-de-la-media-luna-en-san-luis-potosi/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Milenio, Media Luna in Rioverde: how to get there and what to do](https://www.milenio.com/estilo/media-luna-rio-verde-san-luis-potosi-ubicacion-actividades); [México Desconocido, La Media Luna](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/la-media-luna-slp.html)

**CONFIDENCE:** High. Several outlets agree. One July 2026 Tripadvisor visitor reported MXN 150 entry, so readers should still confirm.

### CLAIM 2: Media Luna cabins cost MXN 1,300–2,600 per night with entry included

**CLAIM:** "Cabins MXN 1,300–2,600 per night with entry included."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No source confirmed this range. Older listings give MXN 600–1,700 depending on capacity. Milenio's MXN 1,300 figure is the diving permit, not a cabin. Both mentions (the info box and the FAQ) were softened to "Cabins are also available; ask for current rates by WhatsApp."

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- [Milenio, MXN 1,300 is the diving permit](https://www.milenio.com/estilo/media-luna-rio-verde-san-luis-potosi-ubicacion-actividades)
- [Huasteca Potosina, Laguna de la Media Luna (older cabin prices)](https://huastecapotosina.org/atractivos-turistico/laguna-de-la-media-luna/)

**CONFIDENCE:** Low. No current published rate card was found.

### CLAIM 3: Media Luna's spring water is around 28 °C, and mammoth fossils have been found on its bed

**CLAIM:** "Crystal-clear water that recent visitors measure at around 28 °C… the spring itself is famous for the mammoth fossils divers have found on its bed."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Tourism sources give a year-round 27–30 °C from six thermal springs, and INAH's journal gives about 30 °C. INAH documents underwater archaeology since the 1960s–80s that recovered fossil remains, and divers widely report mammoth bones.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [INAH, Media Luna, a sacred spring in San Luis Potosí (2023)](https://www.revistas.inah.gob.mx/index.php/serie/issue/view/2607)
- **Corroborating Source:** [México Desconocido, La Media Luna](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/la-media-luna-slp.html); [PADI, Manantial Media Luna](https://www.padi.com/zh-hans/dive-site/mo-xi-ge/manantial-media-luna/)

**CONFIDENCE:** High.

### CLAIM 4: Life jackets are mandatory at Media Luna, rental MXN 50

### CLAIM 5: Media Luna allows only ready-made food; grills have been contradictory

**CLAIM:** "Older coverage disagreed on grills (El Universal 2025 listed them as banned; Milenio 2025 listed them as a facility)… in September 2026 the park listed uncooked food as prohibited and allowed only ready-made food."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
El Universal (May 2025) says outside food is allowed only if it is cold and needs no cooking, and that grills are prohibited. Milenio (April 2025) lists grills as available. The post describes this conflict accurately and tells readers to ask first.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal San Luis, Media Luna entry and rules](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/cuanto-cuesta-la-entrada-a-la-laguna-de-la-media-luna-en-san-luis-potosi/)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium.

### CLAIM 6: The Sierra de Álvarez is a federal Flora and Fauna Protection Area of about 16,900 ha

**CLAIM:** "The Sierra de Álvarez Flora and Fauna Protection Area, a federal reserve of about 16,900 hectares."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
CONANP's official file and a 2023 Chamber of Deputies point of agreement both give 16,900 ha. Note that the area still lacks a published management programme, and the post does not claim otherwise.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SEGAM/CONANP file, Sierra de Álvarez](https://slp.gob.mx:443/SEGAM/Documentos%20compartidos/ANP%20FEDERALES/SIERRA%20DE%20ALVAREZ.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Gaceta Parlamentaria, Chamber of Deputies (Nov 2023)](https://gaceta.diputados.gob.mx/Gaceta/65/2023/nov/20231114-III-2.html)

**CONFIDENCE:** High.

### CLAIM 9: Puente de Dios entry MXN 150 (2026), life jackets about MXN 25

**CLAIM:** "Entry is MXN 150 (2026), with life jackets at about MXN 25 and parking at MXN 30."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Visitor reports from April and October 2025 confirm MXN 150 entry. The October 2025 report gives a mandatory life-jacket rental of MXN 50, while other guides cite MXN 30–50. Changed to "about MXN 25–50" in both places (the section and the Pro Tips) to cover the reported range.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Sally Sees, Puente de Dios (Oct 2025: MXN 150 + MXN 50 life jacket)](https://sallysees.com/puente-de-dios/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Travel with Kevin and Ruth (Apr 2025: MXN 150 entry)](https://www.travelwithkevinandruth.com/2025/04/that-was-adventurous-day.html?m=0)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium. These are visitor reports. No official 2026 tariff was found.

### CLAIM 11: SLP's critical wildfire season runs 16 January–30 June; about 23,500 ha had burned in 2026

**CLAIM:** "San Luis Potosí's critical wildfire season runs from 16 January to 30 June. In 2026, around 23,500 hectares had burned by early July."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
El Congresista reports that the critical season ended on 30 June with 87 fires and more than 23,500 ha damaged. N+ cites CONAFOR's 23,550 ha across 87 fires, with the Altiplano worst hit.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [N+, SLP exceeds 23,000 ha burned (CONAFOR data)](https://www.nmas.com.mx/san-luis-potosi/seguridad/san-luis-potosi-supera-las-23-mil-hectareas-afectadas-por-incendios-forestales/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Congresista, wildfire season closes 30 June](https://www.elcongresista.mx/incendios-forestales-san-luis-potosi)

**CONFIDENCE:** High.

### CLAIM 14: Highway 57 around Matehuala was the most-flagged corridor in 2026, including a kidnapping in March 2026

**CLAIM:** "In 2026 the stretch around Matehuala… was the most-flagged corridor in local and national coverage, including a kidnapping in March 2026."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Seven electricians travelling on Highway 57 between Matehuala and Cárdenas were reported missing on 21 March 2026 and found alive on 23 March, the same day a Matehuala security official was arrested. Mexico Daily Post (May 2026) reports growing alarm over the SLP–Matehuala section of Highway 57.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Mexico Daily Post, Highway 57 SLP–Matehuala section: alarm grows (May 2026)](https://mexicodailypost.news/2026/05/01/highway-57-san-luis-potosi-matehuala-section-alarm-grows/)

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium.
