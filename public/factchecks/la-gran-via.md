## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Restaurant still operates in September 2026

**CLAIM:** "La Gran Vía … Discover authentic Spanish cuisine … in San Luis Potosí" (implies an operating restaurant as of the March 2024 post; checked for continued operation as of September 2026)

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
No credible “permanently closed” reports were found. The restaurant’s official website remains live with the Carranza address and phone. Yelp lists the business as updated May 2026 with published hours; Peoople/Google-linked listings show ~996 Google reviews at about 4.6/5 and weekday hours still published.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía official site](https://lagranviaslp.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Yelp — La Gran Via (updated May 2026)](https://www.yelp.com.mx/biz/la-gran-via-san-luis-potos%C3%AD)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Peoople — Restaurante La Gran Vía (Google 4.6/5, ~996 votes)](https://peoople.app/es/restaurantes/restaurante-la-gran-via/b234954d-d9b7-823a-5a79-4234954d6e9a/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
A March 2024 promotional post can go stale if a venue closes. Cross-checks in September 2026 show an active site, listing pages with current-looking hours, and no closed-status flags. Wikivoyage still lists it under “Splurge” dining (last note Oct 2022), consistent with continued recognition rather than closure.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent directories and a live official site agree the venue is open.

### CLAIM 2: Address Av. Venustiano Carranza #560, 78233

**CLAIM:** "Address: Av. Venustiano Carranza #560, 78233 San Luis Potosí"

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Street and number match the official website, Marco Beteta, Yelp, Sección Amarilla, and Wikivoyage. Postal code 78233 appears on the official site; some aggregators show 78000 or 78200, a common Centro CP variance that does not change the physical location.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía official site — location](https://lagranviaslp.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Marco Beteta — La Gran Vía](https://mbmarcobeteta.com/lugares/la-gran-via/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [English Wikivoyage — San Luis Potosí (Splurge)](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/San_Luis_Potos%C3%AD)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Every credible listing converges on Av. Venustiano Carranza 560, Centro Histórico. The article’s CP matches the restaurant’s own site; aggregator CP mismatches are immaterial for navigation.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Official site and independent guides agree on street and number.

### CLAIM 3: Founded in 1979 / culinary tradition since 1979

**CLAIM:** "Founded in 1979, La Gran Vía has been a cornerstone of Spanish cuisine in San Luis Potosí … Culinary tradition since 1979"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The restaurant’s official “Nuestra Historia” states it opened in 1979. Food critic Marco Beteta independently describes it as offering Spanish and international cuisine “desde 1979.” No contradictory founding year was found in public sources.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía official site — opened 1979](https://lagranviaslp.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Marco Beteta — “ofreciendo desde 1979”](https://mbmarcobeteta.com/lugares/la-gran-via/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Founding years for private restaurants rarely appear in government gazettes; the best available primary is the venue’s own history plus a major Mexican food guide repeating 1979. Absence of conflicting dates supports treating 1979 as confirmed for this investigation. The article does not name a specific owner, so no owner-attribution claim was testable.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium-High — Strong self-report plus one independent guide; no archival municipal permit located.

### CLAIM 4: “Over 36 years” / “36 years of experience”

**CLAIM:** "Experience over 36 years of Spanish culinary tradition" / "36 years of experience: Culinary tradition since 1979"

**VERDICT:** 🔄 OUTDATED

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
If founded in 1979, by the March 2024 publish date the restaurant had about 45 years of operation, not “over 36.” By September 2026 it has about 47 years. The “36 years” figure was already stale when the post went live and is more outdated now.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía official site — opened 1979](https://lagranviaslp.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** Article publish metadata (2024-03-20) vs arithmetic from 1979

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
1979 + 36 ≈ 2015. Keeping a static “36 years” slogan after 2015 understates longevity. The underlying “since 1979” claim remains valid; only the year-count packaging is obsolete.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Arithmetic from a corroborated founding year.

### CLAIM 5: Spanish cuisine / authentic Spanish recipes

**CLAIM:** "Authentic Spanish recipes" / "specialized" Spanish cuisine with Mediterranean flavors, paellas, and Spanish regional specialties

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Official branding is “cocina española e internacional.” Marco Beteta, Wikivoyage, Yelp, and Sección Amarilla all categorize it as Spanish (often with international/Mexican fusion dishes). Paella and classic Spanish plates are repeatedly cited.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía official site](https://lagranviaslp.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Marco Beteta — Española, Internacional](https://mbmarcobeteta.com/lugares/la-gran-via/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Wikivoyage — Spanish classics / paella](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/San_Luis_Potos%C3%AD)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Taste judgments (“finest,” “authentic”) are subjective and were not scored. The checkable cuisine-category claim—Spanish (plus international/local fusion)—is consistently supported. “Original Spanish recipes with local ingredients” fits the widely described Spanish–local mix without contradiction.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Category agreement across official and third-party sources.

### CLAIM 6: Hours Monday–Sunday 1:00 PM – 11:00 PM

**CLAIM:** "Monday to Sunday: 1:00 PM - 11:00 PM" / "open daily 1:00 PM–11:00 PM"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
A ~1:00 PM open time is widely confirmed, but a uniform daily close at 11:00 PM is not. Current listings generally show Monday–Saturday roughly 1:00 PM–midnight and a shorter Sunday evening (sources vary between about 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Yelp hours — Mon–Sat 1:00 PM–12:00 AM; Sun 1:00 PM–8:00 PM](https://www.yelp.com.mx/biz/la-gran-via-san-luis-potos%C3%AD)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Marco Beteta — Mon–Sat 13:00–00:00; Sun 13:00–22:00](https://mbmarcobeteta.com/lugares/la-gran-via/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Sección Amarilla — Mon–Sat to 00:00; Sun to 20:00](https://www.seccionamarilla.com.mx/informacion/restaurante-la-gran-via/equipos-para-restaurantes/san-luis-potosi/san-luis-potosi/centro/400259439)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article’s open-at-1:00 PM element holds. The 11:00 PM close-every-day schedule understates typical weekday closing (midnight) and oversimplifies Sunday. Call ahead for Sunday hours; aggregator Sunday closes disagree slightly.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Strong consensus on weekday pattern; Sunday close time inconsistent across sources.

### CLAIM 7: Named one of Mexico’s 100 must-visit gastronomy places in 2017

**CLAIM:** "In 2017, La Gran Vía received the prestigious recognition of being named one of Mexico's 100 must-visit places in the gastronomy category"

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The restaurant’s own menu page claims a 2017 “100 Imperdibles” gastronomy distinction and links to the contest’s Facebook presence. The 100 Imperdibles program is real, but no independent 2017 winners archive listing La Gran Vía was located during this investigation.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [La Gran Vía menu page — self-reported 2017 honor](https://lagranviaslp.com/menu/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [100 Imperdibles de México — program bases](https://www.100imperdiblesmx.com/bases)
- **Counter-Evidence / Gap:** Public news roundups of later editions do not reproduce a 2017 gastronomy winners list naming La Gran Vía

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Self-promotion is not enough under the investigation standard when a primary organizer archive is missing. The claim may be true, but without an independent or organizer winner list it cannot be confirmed. Treated as unverifiable rather than false.

**CONFIDENCE:** Low — Only the restaurant’s marketing page supports the specific 2017 win.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Identity and location facts (open status, address, founding year, Spanish cuisine category, main phone) hold up. Soft marketing numbers (“36 years”) and contact/email packaging are where the post drifts. Hours are directionally right on open time but wrong as a flat daily close.

### BIAS INDICATORS
The piece reads as venue-adjacent promotional copy (first-person “our,” award emphasis, “finest” framing) rather than neutral reporting. That does not make the core facts false, but it explains unverifiable prestige claims and stale year-counts left unupdated.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Best anchors are lagranviaslp.com, Marco Beteta, Wikivoyage, Yelp (May 2026 update), and Sección Amarilla. Weakest link is the 2017 award, which lacks an independent winners archive in open web sources checked on this date.
