## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Capital Coffee address and operating status

**CLAIM:** "Capital Coffee is at Avenida Cuauhtémoc 1327, Jardín, San Luis Potosí."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Multiple independent directories list Capital Coffee SLP at Av. Cuauhtémoc 1327, Jardín (C.P. 78270), with published hours roughly 8:00 AM–10:00 PM daily and phone +52 444 246 8714. No credible reports of closure between the December 2025 article date and September 2026.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Capital Coffee SLP — Restaurantes México](https://restaurantes-mexico.com.mx/capital-coffee-slp-san-luis-potosi/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Capital Coffee Slp — Nearby Restaurant profile](https://capital-coffee-slp.nearby-res.com/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Address, colonia Jardín, and current operating listings align with the article. Hours were not asserted as a hard claim in the post; aggregator hours are consistent across sources. No price figures were stated in the article for this venue.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Address and open status multiply corroborated; no closure signal found.

### CLAIM 2: Café Sideral has two locations in San Luis Potosí

**CLAIM:** "Café Sideral — Address: Two locations in San Luis Potosí (check their website)."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Café Sideral clearly operates at Hermenegildo Galeana 475, Centro Histórico (owner Mauricio Montiel confirmed this address in an ERRR Magazine interview; delivery apps still list it). A local LíderLife piece once described a second branch on Fray Diego de la Magdalena, but as of September 2026 no independent directory, delivery listing, or owner statement confirms a second active café address.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Café Sideral — ERRR Magazine interview](https://errr-magazine.com/cafe-sideral/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Café Sideral — Rappi (Galeana 475)](https://www.rappi.com.mx/restaurantes/1923233862-cafe-sideral)
- **Older dual-location mention:** [LíderLife — pet-friendly cafés SLP](https://liderlife.liderempresarial.com/cafeterias-pet-friendly-para-disfrutar-en-slp/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Existence and Centro location are solid. The article’s “two locations” claim is only weakly supported and not currently verifiable as both operating. This is not scored OUTDATED because the second address cannot be confirmed as having closed after December 2025—only that dual operation is not substantiated now. Self-description as “el café más bonito” is branding, not fact-checked.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — One active location high-confidence; second location unresolved.

### CLAIM 4: 500 Noches originated in Tuxtla Gutiérrez, Chiapas

**CLAIM:** "Originally from Tuxtla Gutiérrez, Chiapas, 500 Noches brings southern Mexican warmth to San Luis Potosí."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Grupo 500 Noches’ own corporate history states the first Café Bar 500 Noches opened in Tuxtla Gutiérrez in April 2011. An academic thesis on the group likewise records founding in Tuxtla Gutiérrez in 2011 before later expansion (including San Cristóbal de las Casas).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Grupo Quinientas Noches — LinkedIn company history](https://www.linkedin.com/in/grupo-quinientas-noches-8054562b0)
- **Corroborating Source:** [UPC thesis on Grupo 500 Noches (hdl.handle.net/10757/671028)](http://hdl.handle.net/10757/671028)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article correctly identifies Tuxtla Gutiérrez as the brand origin. Later HQ/operations in San Cristóbal do not contradict the founding claim.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Corporate and academic sources align on 2011 Tuxtla founding.

### CLAIM 5: 500 Noches breakfast hours 9 AM–1 PM daily

**CLAIM:** "Breakfast served 9 AM to 1 PM daily."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Venue opening time of ~9:00 AM daily is widely reported and fits a breakfast start. Full published hours run roughly 9:00 AM–1:00 AM, so the café is open far past 1:00 PM. No primary menu schedule found that independently confirms breakfast service *ends* at 1:00 PM every day.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [AAA TripCanvas hours — Diario 09:00–01:00](https://www.aaa.com/tripcanvas/restaurant/500-noches-san-luis-AA74693)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Peoople — Café Bar 500 Noches San Luis hours](https://peoople.app/pt/restaurantes/cafe-bar-500-noches-san-luis/48ad95a8-2b74-396d-5ab2-b8ad95a8e2b8/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Core idea (breakfast from 9 AM) is plausible and consistent with opening time. The exclusive 9 AM–1 PM breakfast window is not corroborated as an official meal-period cutoff. Live-music “nightly” / Wednesday tribute specifics were treated as soft programming claims and not scored separately; trova/tribute culture is brand-typical but schedule currency was not verified from a primary SLP calendar for September 2026.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — Open-at-9 confirmed; breakfast end-time not primary-sourced.

### CLAIM 6: Las Castañas addresses (Tequis and Centro)

**CLAIM:** "Las Castañas — Av. Venustiano Carranza 1325, Tequisquiapan (and Centro at Independencia #800)."

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Las Castañas — Contacto (Carranza 1325)](https://lascastanas.com/pages/contact)

- **Corroborating Source:** [Las Castañas — Políticas / Independencia #800](https://lascastanas.com/policies/contact-information)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Both street addresses match. “Across from Tequis Church” is geographically consistent with Parroquia / Iglesia de Tequis listings near Carranza ~1300. No closure of either branch found after December 2025.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Official site plus delivery platforms confirm both addresses.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Street-level addresses for four of five venues are accurate and still valid nine months after publication. Brand origin (500 Noches / Tuxtla) and Las Castañas dual branches are well sourced. Weakest factual layer is incomplete location specificity for Café Sideral and meal-window precision at 500 Noches.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Ranking and “cozy” language are promotional (excluded from scoring). Follower counts and Instagram-worthiness are used as social-proof devices but the numeric claim checked out.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Strongest evidence came from official venue/group sites (Las Castañas, Grupo 500 Noches, Casa H) plus delivery/travel directories. Secondary lifestyle listings (LíderLife) were useful for leads but insufficient alone for dual-location confirmation.
