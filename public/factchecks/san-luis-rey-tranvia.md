## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: San Luis Rey is a tourist tram/trolley of the historic center

**CLAIM:** "San Luis Rey offers traditional trolley tours of San Luis Potosí’s UNESCO World Heritage historic center" / "traditional San Luis Rey trolley tours."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
San Luis Rey is the city’s commercial tourist tram (also called Turibús/tranvía turístico). The operator and local press describe guided circuit tours of the Centro Histórico aboard open tourist trolleys, in collaboration with state tourism and the municipal government.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Tranvía Turístico San Luis Rey — official site](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — Recorre el Centro Histórico en tranvía (17 May 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Identity, product type (tourist tram), and historic-center focus match primary and 2026 local coverage. Marketing language (“perfect,” “preferred choice”) was not scored as a factual claim.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — operator branding plus consistent local reporting.

### CLAIM 2: Historic center is a UNESCO World Heritage site

**CLAIM:** "UNESCO World Heritage historic center" / "The historic center of San Luis Potosí is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
UNESCO lists the Historic centre of the city of San Luis Potosí as component 1351-039 of the serial World Heritage property Camino Real de Tierra Adentro (inscribed 2010). Framing it as UNESCO heritage for the centro histórico is correct; it is not a standalone single-property inscription under only the city name.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [UNESCO — Camino Real de Tierra Adentro (list 1351)](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1351/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [UNESCO maps — component 1351-039 Historic centre of the city of San Luis Potosí](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1351/maps/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [INAH — San Luis Potosí / Camino Real context](https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/en/node/4813)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The article’s shorthand is acceptable for visitors. Strictly, the inscription is the Camino Real serial property, of which SLP’s historic centre is one component (~70 ha).

**CONFIDENCE:** High — UNESCO primary documentation.

### CLAIM 4: Operates every day of the year (Monday–Sunday)

**CLAIM:** "every day of the year" / schedule rows "Monday to Sunday" / "Daily Departures."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Sources consistently say the service runs Monday through Sunday within a daytime window. Absolute “every day of the year” (including weather suspensions, private charters, or unannounced closures) is not guaranteed by the operator’s public copy; the article itself notes weather conditions.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Official site — De 10:00 am a 7:30 pm, salidas cada 25 minutos](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — lunes a domingo, 10:00–19:30 (May 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Core claim of daily (Mon–Sun) public operation is supported. The stronger “every day of the year” wording overstates certainty; treat as regular daily service subject to weather/operations.

**CONFIDENCE:** Medium — strong for Mon–Sun; weak for literal 365-day guarantee.

### CLAIM 5: Tour duration is 45 minutes

**CLAIM:** "45-minute departures" / schedule table "Duration … 45 minutes" / "45-minute guided tour."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Independent local reporting describes an approximately one-and-a-half-hour circuit, not 45 minutes. No operator page consulted states a 45-minute standard tour length for the regular historic route.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [El Universal San Luis — “duración aproximada de hora y media” (23 Apr 2025)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/estos-son-los-sitios-del-centro-historico-que-puedes-conocer-en-el-turibus-san-luis-rey/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — “recorrido de una hora y media” (14 Apr 2024)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/turibus-de-san-luis-descubre-la-capital-a-traves-de-sus-calles-e-historia-este-es-el-recorrido-y-costo/)
- **Counter-Evidence:** Article tables repeatedly list 45 minutes with no matching operator confirmation.

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
A ~90-minute narrated loop through Carmen, cathedral corridor, San Francisco, San Miguelito, Guadalupe, etc., matches published stop lists far better than a 45-minute run. Verdict is FALSE on the stated duration.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — two independent El Universal pieces across years agree on ~90 minutes.

### CLAIM 6: Fixed departures at 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM (plus 6:00 PM Fri–Sun)

**CLAIM:** Schedule table listing only 10:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM Monday–Sunday and 6:00 PM Friday–Sunday.

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The operator and 2026 press describe continuous departures about every 25 minutes from 10:00 a.m. to 7:30 p.m., seven days a week—not a four-slot fixed timetable. The article’s 6:00 PM Friday–Sunday-only slot is not how the service is advertised.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Official site — 10:00 am–7:30 pm, salidas cada 25 minutos](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — May 2026: 10:00–19:30, cada 25 minutos](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Candidato MX — same frequency model (27 Mar 2024)](https://elcandidato.mx/2024/03/27/recorre-cada-rincon-de-slp-y-conoce-su-historia-a-bordo-del-turibus/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Visitors following only the article’s clock times would miss most departures. Service-hours line “9:30 AM – 6:30 PM” in the article also conflicts with the operator’s 10:00–19:30 window.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — operator homepage matches 2026 local reporting.

### CLAIM 7: Adult ticket price is $80 MXN

**CLAIM:** "for $80 MXN" / schedule table Price "$80 MXN" for all listed departures.

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
As of verification (21 Sep 2026), the operator lists **$150 MXN adults** and **$100 MXN children & INAPAM**, with tickets sold on the trams. El Universal San Luis (17 May 2026) repeats those figures. Earlier coverage (2024–April 2025) documented **$120** adults / **$100** preferential—not $80. No credible source found that ever set the general adult fare at $80 MXN, so this is FALSE rather than OUTDATED.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Official site — $150 Adultos / $100 Niños e INAPAM](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — $150 / $100 (17 May 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)
- **Prior fare context:** [El Universal San Luis — $120 / $100 (23 Apr 2025)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/estos-son-los-sitios-del-centro-historico-que-puedes-conocer-en-el-turibus-san-luis-rey/)
- **Prior fare context:** [El Universal San Luis — $120 / $100 (14 Apr 2024)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/turibus-de-san-luis-descubre-la-capital-a-traves-de-sus-calles-e-historia-este-es-el-recorrido-y-costo/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Price history for the general adult ticket: ~$120 (2024–early 2025) → $150 (operator + May 2026 press). The article’s $80 figure does not match any verified stage of that history. A private “Recorrido Completo” listing at 240 MXN / 2 h on the operator’s booking page is a different product, not the standard hop-on circuit.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — 2026 operator + contemporaneous press; multi-year price trail excludes $80.

### CLAIM 8: No reservation required; tickets sold on the trolley

**CLAIM:** "No reservation required" / "purchase your tickets directly at the trolley" / "No prior reservation required."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Operator and press agree that regular-route tickets are sold directly on the vehicles at Jardín San Juan de Dios. Separate phone/web booking exists for private, school, and special packages, which does not negate walk-up boarding for the standard circuit.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Official site — “Los boletos se venden directamente en los tranvías”](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — venta directa en los tranvías (May 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Claim is accurate for the public historic route. Group/private hires still need advance contact (e.g., 4441100033 per press).

**CONFIDENCE:** High — explicit operator statement.

### CLAIM 9: Special prices — under 3 free, students $60, groups of 10+ get 15% off

**CLAIM:** "Children under 3 years: Free • Students with ID: $60 MXN • Groups of 10+: 15% discount."

**VERDICT:** ❓ UNVERIFIABLE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Published fares emphasize adults $150 and children/INAPAM $100. Press notes special student, convention, and private packages exist, but no primary source published the exact $60 student fare, free-under-3 rule, or 15% group discount. A flat “children free under 3” claim conflicts with the advertised child fare of $100 unless an unstated toddler exception exists.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Official site — $150 / $100 Niños e INAPAM only](https://www.tranviasanluisrey.com/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — special student/convention/private rates mentioned without those numbers (May 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/recorre-slp-en-tranvia-costos-y-recorrido/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Discount *categories* (students, groups) are plausible; the article’s specific numbers are not corroborated. Not scored FALSE because private tariff sheets may exist offline, but they cannot be confirmed from public sources.

**CONFIDENCE:** Low — absence of published figures; child $100 undercuts “under 3 free” as stated.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Identity, UNESCO status, boarding point, and walk-up ticketing hold up. Operational numbers that matter to visitors—duration, clock schedule, and adult price—are wrong relative to the operator and 2026 press.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Promotional tone (preferred choice, unforgettable, Distintivo M / SECTUR “official recognition”) without evidence; Distintivo M was not verified for this operator and was not scored as a separate claim.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Best evidence is the operator homepage plus El Universal San Luis May 2026. Older El Universal pieces document the prior $120 adult fare. UNESCO WHC confirms heritage status. No city tourism PDF with a 2026 tariff board was located beyond press citing the operator.
