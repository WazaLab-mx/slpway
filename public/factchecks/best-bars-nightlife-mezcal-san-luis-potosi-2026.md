## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: La Piquería Mezcalería at Independencia 1190

**CLAIM:** "For mezcal head to La Piquería… Independencia 1190, Centro… tables looking straight at the baroque Capilla de Aranzazú… champions potosino mezcal."

**VERDICT:** 🔄 OUTDATED

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The address, Aranzazú plaza location, and former role as a local mezcalería are well documented. Multiple SLP outlets reported a definitive closure in June 2024 after about 14 years under that name. The article (published July 2026) still presents it as an open destination.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Pulso SLP — Anuncian cierre de La Piquería (22 Jun 2024)](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/slp/anuncian-cierre-de-la-piqueria-en-el-centro-historico/1810868)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal San Luis — Cierra La Piquería (30 Jun 2024)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/decidimos-terminar-este-viaje-cierra-la-piqueria-famosa-mezcaleria-de-san-luis/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Time Out México — historic listing Independencia 1190](https://www.timeoutmexico.mx/ciudad-de-mexico/bares-y-cantinas/la-piqueria-mezcaleria)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Independencia 1190 / Independencia y Melchor Ocampo frente a Plaza Aranzazú matches the article. No credible source found that La Piquería reopened under that name after mid-2024. Recommending it in a 2026 nightlife guide is therefore stale guidance, not a false historical address.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Closure covered by independent local press with the venue’s own farewell statement.

Callejón 7B is an operating craft-beer bar at Avenida Universidad 153, Centro. Business directories and restaurant listings consistently show that street number and phone, tied to Cervezas Artesanales Potosinas / Siete Barrios.

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Existence and address match. The article’s separate “20+ taps” line is softer (reviews cite 20+ beer types; draft/tap count is not independently locked), but the address/venue claim itself holds.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Multiple independent listings agree on the street number.

### CLAIM 4: Bar Tampico is around 80 years old

**CLAIM:** "Bar Tampico (~80 years)… Around eighty years old and famous for its vaulted brick basement."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Local heritage and press place Bar Tampico on Los Bravo in the Centro, commonly dating the business to 1941 (~85 years by September 2026). The owner has also described it as about 80 years old. The vaulted basement / tunnel legend is widely repeated as local lore.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Patrimonio San Luis — Bar Tampico desde 1941](http://patrimoniosanluis.com/2022/09/13/bar-tampico-historia-y-tradicion-desde-1941/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Orquesta — cantinas antiguas; propietario ~80 años; Los Bravo 205](https://laorquesta.mx/estilodevida-las-cantinas-mas-antiguas-de-san-luis-potosi/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Sol de San Luis — Bar Tampico desde 1941](https://oem.com.mx/elsoldesanluis/local/bar-tampico-calmando-la-sed-desde-1941-17275400)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
“~80 years” is a fair round figure relative to 1941 (and matches the owner’s quoted age). One family account argues the premises may be older than 100 years; that does not falsify the article’s approximate claim. Article omits the street address (Los Bravo 205) but does not invent a wrong one.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Converging local sources on 1941 / ~80-year framing.

The 130+ age claim itself is supported.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**

### CLAIM 8: Legal drinking age is 18

**CLAIM:** "Legal drinking age is 18 and IDs are checked at clubs."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The Ley de Bebidas Alcohólicas del Estado de San Luis Potosí treats supplying alcohol to persons under eighteen as a criminal offense and requires ID checks for entry to bars, cervecerías, nightclubs, and pulquerías. Federal practice nationwide is likewise 18+.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Congreso SLP — Ley de Bebidas Alcohólicas (texto vigente; menores de dieciocho)](http://congresosanluis.gob.mx/sites/default/files/unpload/legislacion/leyes/2025/08/Ley%20de%20Bebidas%20Alcoholicas%20del%20Estado%20de%20San%20Luis%20Potosi%20%20%28al%2004%20Sept%202024%29.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [FAOLEX mirror — same statute](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mex229996.pdf)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Age-18 rule is explicit in state alcohol law. Club ID checks are required by statute for those venue classes, not merely a nightlife tip.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Primary statute.

### CLAIM 9: Bars generally run about 8pm to 2am

**CLAIM:** "Cantinas open in the afternoon; bars generally run 8pm to 2am; clubs are a Thursday-to-Saturday affair…"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
State law caps bar/brewery alcohol service roughly 10:00–2:00 (municipal schedules within those limits); nightclubs generally 21:00–2:00. Closing near 2am is therefore a real legal outer bound. Opening “generally 8pm” is too narrow: many listed venues (La Histórica, La Oruga, Bolívar) open early afternoon, and cantinas are correctly described as afternoon institutions.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Ley de Bebidas Alcohólicas SLP, Art. 27 — bares 10:00–2:00; centros nocturnos 21:00–2:00](http://congresosanluis.gob.mx/sites/default/files/unpload/legislacion/leyes/2025/08/Ley%20de%20Bebidas%20Alcoholicas%20del%20Estado%20de%20San%20Luis%20Potosi%20%20%28al%2004%20Sept%202024%29.pdf)
- **Corroborating Source:** [lahistorica.mx hours — from 2:00 PM](https://lahistorica.mx/es/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [laorugaylacebada.com.mx hours — from noon; closes by midnight](https://laorugaylacebada.com.mx/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Core rhythm (afternoon cantinas; late nights peaking Thu–Sat; alcohol often until ~2am) is directionally right. Framing ordinary “bars” as 8pm–2am understates both the legal daytime window and published hours of venues the article itself recommends.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Statute plus venue hours.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Street numbers for active Universidad and Centro venues (7B, Oruga, Bolívar, Histórica) and historic age ranges for Tampico and Peñasquito are strong. Legal age is correctly stated. The largest factual failure is treating a closed mezcalería as current.

### BIAS INDICATORS
Tone is promotional (“best,” “soul of,” “must”) but the checkable layer is mostly addresses and ages rather than fabricated stats. No evidence of intentional deception; the La Piquería problem looks like unupdated local knowledge.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Original article cites no primary sources. Verification relied on venue sites, SLP Congress statute text, and local newspapers (Pulso, El Universal San Luis, La Orquesta, Patrimonio San Luis).
