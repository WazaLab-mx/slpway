## DETAILED FINDINGS

### CLAIM 1: Semana Santa cultural window March 27–April 11; Good Friday April 3; Easter Sunday April 5

**CLAIM:** "Cultural activities run from March 27 to April 11, 2026. The key dates are Good Friday (April 3 — Procesión del Silencio) and Easter Sunday (April 5)."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Good Friday on April 3 and Easter Sunday on April 5, 2026 are correct and match the liturgical calendar used statewide. A Pascua cultural wrap through April 11 is supported (Pulso SLP; Encuentro de Diablos at Museo Nacional de la Máscara on April 11). Several SECULT-linked announcements framed the main 100+ activity block as March 27–April 5, while Sectur’s post-season tourism window was March 28–April 12 — so “March 27–April 11” is close but not the only official framing.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SECULT — Procesión del Silencio 2026 (Apr 3, 20:00)](https://cultura.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/4/2/procesi%C3%B3n-del-silencio-2026/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Pulso SLP — Programa cultural de Semana Santa y Pascua (ends Apr 11)](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/cultura/programa-cultural-de-semana-santa-y-pascua--/2031873)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Potosinoticias — Más de 100 actividades (Mar 27–Apr 5)](https://potosinoticias.com/2026/03/23/mas-de-100-actividades-marcaran-la-semana-santa-2026-en-slp/)
- **Post-event tourism window:** [El Economista — derrama Mar 28–Apr 12](https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/politica/san-luis-potosi-alcanza-derrama-historica-semana-santa-pascua-1-280-millones-pesos-20260413-808522.html)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The liturgical anchors the article uses for visitors (Good Friday / Easter) are solid. The broader “Mar 27–Apr 11” band mixes SECULT’s early cultural start with Pascua programming that did run into mid-April, but it does not match Sectur’s later tourism accounting window exactly and overlaps with a wrong March 27 inauguration claim treated separately below.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — liturgical dates and Apr 11 Pascua events are well documented; exact window wording varies by agency.

### CLAIM 2: 73rd Procesión del Silencio on Good Friday, April 3, 2026, 8:00 PM, from Templo del Carmen

**CLAIM:** "Held on Good Friday (April 3, 2026)… Templo del Carmen, 8:00 PM — The main event. 73rd edition…"

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
SECULT listed the event for Friday April 3, 2026 at 20:00 in the Centro Histórico. Pre-event coverage (Potosinoticias, El Universal) and post-event reporting (El Radar, Apr 4) confirm the 73rd edition started at 20:00 from Templo del Carmen / Plaza del Carmen and that Governor Gallardo participated.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SECULT — Procesión del Silencio 2026](https://cultura.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/4/2/procesi%C3%B3n-del-silencio-2026/) — 3 de abril de 2026, 20:00
- **Corroborating Source:** [Potosinoticias — Presentan la LXXIII Procesión (Mar 13, 2026)](https://potosinoticias.com/2026/03/13/video-presentan-la-lxxiii-procesion-del-silencio-en-san-luis-potosi/) — salida del Templo de Nuestra Señora del Carmen
- **Post-event:** [El Radar — Gallardo participa en la Procesión (Apr 4, 2026)](https://elradar.mx/ultimas-noticias/ricardo-gallardo-participa-en-la-procesion-del-silencio-simbolo-de-identidad-en-slp/) — inició a las 20:00 en el Templo del Carmen

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
This is the article’s strongest claim cluster. Date, hour, starting church, and edition number align across official culture listings and after-the-fact coverage. No credible source places the 2026 capital procession on a different day or start time.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Tier-2 official listing plus same-day/next-day independent confirmation.

### CLAIM 3: 4,000+ hooded participants and 32 cofradías

**CLAIM:** "4,000+ participants, 32 cofradías" / "4,000+ hooded participants marching in absolute silence."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The cofradía count is in the right range: Tradiciones Potosinas / El Universal and Canal 7 cited 32 cofradías before the event; post-event government-linked copy referred to about 30. Participant figures repeatedly cite around 2,000 cofrades/penitents — not 4,000+. Post-event El Radar explicitly says “2 mil penitentes.”

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary/near-primary:** [El Universal SLP — ~2 mil cofrades, 32 cofradías (Mar 13, 2026)](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/procesion-del-silencio-2026-todo-sobre-la-celebracion-de-semana-santa-en-slp/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [La Jornada San Luis — más de dos mil cofrades, más de 30 cofradías](https://lajornadasanluis.com.mx/slp-fortalece-la-tradicion-de-la-procesion-del-silencio/)
- **Post-event:** [El Radar — 2 mil penitentes, 30 cofradías (Apr 4, 2026)](https://elradar.mx/ultimas-noticias/ricardo-gallardo-participa-en-la-procesion-del-silencio-simbolo-de-identidad-en-slp/)
- **Counter-evidence:** A minority of outlets floated higher cofradía counts (e.g., “40”); those conflict with the Tradiciones Potosinas presentation and are not used here as controlling.

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Doubling the marching contingent from ~2,000 to 4,000+ is a material error. Keeping “32 cofradías” is fair within reporting noise (30–32). Attendance expectations of 100,000–120,000 (or up to 150,000) refer to spectators, not participants, and should not be conflated with the 4,000+ figure.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — multiple independent pre- and post-event sources converge on ~2,000 participants.

### CLAIM 4: Altar de Dolores inauguration launched celebrations on March 27 at Palacio de Gobierno

**CLAIM:** "The celebration was officially launched on March 27 with the inauguration of the Altar de la Virgen de los Dolores at the Palacio de Gobierno." / calendar: "Mar 27 Altar de Dolores Inauguration."

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Governor Ricardo Gallardo inaugurated the Altar de la Virgen de los Dolores at Palacio de Gobierno on March 25, 2026 (Viernes de Dolores timing), not March 27. Multiple outlets dated the inauguration March 25; El Universal covered the installed altar on March 26.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary reporting:** [Realidad San Luis — inauguración March 25, 2026](https://realidadsanluis.com/2026/03/25/palacio-de-gobierno-abre-sus-puertas-al-altar-de-dolores-inaugurado-por-ricardo-gallardo/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Global Media — miércoles 25 marzo, 2026](https://www.globalmedia.mx/articles/gobierno_estatal_inaugura_altar_de_la_virgen_de_dolores)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Revista Punto de Vista — March 25, 2026](https://revistapuntodevista.com.mx/nflash/ricardo-gallardo-inaugura-altar-de-dolores-en-palacio-de-gobierno/900700/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Location and tradition framing (Palacio de Gobierno altar) are correct; the launch date is two days late. The article’s “Mar 27 start” narrative appears to have been anchored to this wrong inauguration date rather than the actual Viernes de Dolores ceremony.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — consistent same-day multi-outlet dating to March 25.

### CLAIM 5: Festival San Luis en Primavera March 28–April 4 — 110+ events, 800+ artists, 14 venues, free admission

**CLAIM:** "Mar 28 - Apr 4 Festival San Luis en Primavera — 110+ events, 800+ artists, 14 venues… Free admission."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The municipal festival site lists March 28–April 4, 2026. Opening coverage quotes Mayor Galindo on 110+ events across 14 venues with 800+ artists. The official FAQ states the great majority of activities are free and open-access (capacity limits for indoor venues such as Teatro de la Paz).

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Ayuntamiento — Festival San Luis en Primavera 2026](https://sitio.sanluis.gob.mx/FestivalSanLuisEnPrimavera/) — 28 de marzo al 4 de abril de 2026
- **Primary Source:** [Festival FAQ — majority free / open access](https://sitio.sanluis.gob.mx/FestivalSanLuisEnPrimavera/FAQ)
- **Corroborating Source:** [sanluisinforma — >110 eventos, 14 sedes, >800 artistas](https://sanluisinforma.com/?p=18546)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Counts and dates match municipal messaging. “Free admission” is accurate for plaza programming; the FAQ’s caveat about limited indoor capacity does not contradict the article’s free framing.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — official municipal site plus contemporaneous local coverage.

### CLAIM 6: Most Semana Santa events free; Procesión del Silencio watchable free or reserved seats $77–$297 MXN

**CLAIM:** "Most events are free, including the Festival de Primavera, Vía Crucis reenactments, altar exhibitions, and Copa Potosí. The Procesión del Silencio can be watched for free or with reserved seating ($77-$297 MXN)."

**VERDICT:** ✅ TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Festival FAQ and Copa Potosí coverage confirm free general admission. Street viewing of the Procesión is free; paid chair zones for 2026 ran from 77 to 297 pesos (El Universal price-trend piece; Fábrica de Periodismo zone list). Early March organizers still quoted 70–270 in some stories; final 2026 band of 77–297 matches the article.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [Festival FAQ — majority free](https://sitio.sanluis.gob.mx/FestivalSanLuisEnPrimavera/FAQ)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — sillas 2026 entre 77 y 297 pesos](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/economia-y-negocios/procesion-del-silencio-de-slp-las-sillas-para-los-espectadores-casi-duplican-su-precio-en-los-ultimos-cuatro-anos/)
- **Corroborating Source:** [Fábrica de Periodismo — zone prices Café $77 … VIP $297](https://fabricadeperiodismo.com/noticias/procesion-del-silencio-slp-2026-horario-ruta-boletos-y-donde-ver/)
- **Copa free admission (with caveats):** [Pulso SLP — acceso general gratuito; VIP/parking cobros](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/slp/palcos-vip-copa-potosi-cobros/2033736)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
The FAQ-style free-events claim holds. Minor nuance: Copa Potosí had free match entry but reported parking and unofficial VIP scaffolding charges — still consistent with “free admission” as commonly marketed.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — price band corroborated by economic reporting and a detailed zone guide.

### CLAIM 7: April 4 — Fiesta de la Luz & Quema de Judas at Catedral Metropolitana & Centro Histórico

**CLAIM:** "Apr 4 Fiesta de la Luz & Quema de Judas — Catedral Metropolitana & Centro Histórico — Cathedral light ceremony and traditional burning of Judas effigies." / "On Easter Saturday…"

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Quema de Judas on Sábado de Gloria (April 4, 2026) did happen in the Centro Histórico (Plaza del Carmen / Museo de la Máscara area), confirmed by SECULT’s event page and same-day Secult success coverage. Fiesta de la Luz videomapping on the Catedral ran March 30–April 2 (19:00–21:30), not April 4 — so pairing both on Apr 4 at the Cathedral is wrong.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary Source:** [SECULT — Quema de Judas, 4 de abril 2026, 19:00, Plaza del Carmen](https://cultura.slp.gob.mx/eventos/2026/4/2/quema-de-judas/)
- **Post-event:** [Diverso Informativo — Quema de Judas success Apr 4 in Plaza del Carmen](https://diversoinformativo.com/estado-impulsa-tradiciones-con-quema-de-judas-y-danza-azteca/)
- **Fiesta de Luz dates:** [Visit México — Fiesta de Luz Mar 30–Apr 2](https://visitmexico.com/es/actividad/763/fiesta-de-luz)
- **Corroborating Source:** [El Universal SLP — proyecciones del 30 de marzo al 2 de abril](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/fiesta-de-luz-en-san-luis-potosi-horarios-del-videomapping-en-la-catedral/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Easter Saturday Quema timing is correct; venue emphasis should be Plaza del Carmen rather than Catedral. Bundling Fiesta de la Luz onto April 4 misstates the light show’s actual run.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — official Quema listing plus clear multi-source Fiesta de Luz schedule.

### CLAIM 8: Over $1.2 billion MXN economic activity and 150K+ expected visitors

**CLAIM:** "…expected to generate over $1.2 billion MXN in economic activity" / glance box "150K+ Expected Visitors."

**VERDICT:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY TRUE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
The ~$1.2B projection floated before and during Holy Week was essentially met: Sectur later reported about $1,280 million MXN for Semana Santa y Pascua (March 28–April 12). Statewide visitor volume was far higher than 150K — more than 870,000 people across the state’s destinations. The 100K–150K band matches Procesión attendance expectations, not statewide tourism; the glance box presents “150K+” as overall Semana Santa visitors.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Pre-event projection:** [Realidad San Luis — Gallardo: más de mil 200 millones (Mar 25)](https://realidadsanluis.com/2026/03/25/palacio-de-gobierno-abre-sus-puertas-al-altar-de-dolores-inaugurado-por-ricardo-gallardo/)
- **Post-event official figure:** [El Economista — 1,280 mdp; >870 mil visitantes (Apr 13, 2026)](https://www.eleconomista.com.mx/politica/san-luis-potosi-alcanza-derrama-historica-semana-santa-pascua-1-280-millones-pesos-20260413-808522.html)
- **Procesión attendance expectation:** [El Universal — 100–120 mil](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/mas-de-san-luis/procesion-del-silencio-2026-todo-sobre-la-celebracion-de-semana-santa-en-slp/); [Policiaca San Luis — 100–150 mil](https://policiacasanluis.com/2026/03/13/esperan-hasta-150-mil-visitantes-en-la-procesion-del-silencio/)

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
As a forward-looking economic claim published April 2, $1.2B was reasonable and later validated. The visitor figure is scope-confused: accurate as a Procesión crowd estimate, misleading as total Semana Santa tourism for SLP.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — Sectur post-season numbers are widely republished; Procesión expectation band is consistent across local press.

### CLAIM 10: Procesión de los Cristos in Villa de Pozos on April 2

**CLAIM:** "Apr 2 Procesión de los Cristos — Villa de Pozos — Solemn procession with religious images…"

**VERDICT:** ❌ FALSE

**INVESTIGATION SUMMARY:**
Contemporary cultural programming placed the Procesión de los Cristos in Villa de Pozos on Good Friday, April 3 at 18:00 — not April 2. April 2 at that parish was a documentary screening (“La Fiesta de los Cristos”), which may explain the calendar slip.

**EVIDENCE CHAIN:**
- **Primary reporting:** [Pulso SLP — Apr 3, 18:00 Procesión de los Cristos en Villa de Pozos; Apr 2 documentary at 17:30](https://pulsoslp.com.mx/cultura/programa-cultural-de-semana-santa-y-pascua--/2031873)
- **Corroborating context:** Same Pulso schedule lists Apr 3 San Juan de Guadalupe Passion at 15:00 and capital Procesión del Silencio at 20:00 — aligning the Cristos procession with Good Friday.

**DETAILED ANALYSIS:**
Naming Villa de Pozos is correct; the date is wrong by one day and conflicts with the article’s own Good Friday emphasis elsewhere. No post-event source was found placing the 2026 Cristos procession on Thursday April 2.

**CONFIDENCE:** High — detailed day-by-day local cultural agenda; no contradicting official listing found for Apr 2.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS

### ACCURACY PATTERNS
Liturgical anchors, Procesión start time/place/edition, Festival de Primavera scale, free-access framing, seat prices, and Copa Potosí logistics are largely accurate. Errors cluster in calendar secondary events (Altar launch day, Cristos procession day, Fiesta de la Luz date) and in inflated/ambiguous crowd metrics (participants vs spectators vs statewide visitors).

### BIAS INDICATORS
Tourism-forward tone (economic impact, visitor expectations, “top destination”) mirrors state and municipal promotion language but does not fabricate the Procesión’s core schedule. The main risk is promotional rounding (4,000+ marchers; 150K “visitors”) rather than partisan distortion.

### SOURCING QUALITY
Strongest verification came from SECULT event pages, municipal Festival site/FAQ, INPODE, Sectur post-season tourism figures, and next-day procession coverage. Weaker spots are where the article compressed multiple Centro Histórico traditions onto a single calendar cell without checking updated light-show dates.
