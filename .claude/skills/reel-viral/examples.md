# reel-viral — worked example

## Reel: "El Gaaas" (street sounds of SLP)

Hook (on-screen, frame 1): **"Month 1 in San Luis Potosí"**
Concept: The gas-truck jingle plays outside; in month 1 the newcomer panics, by month 12 they sing along and sprint out with the tank.
Why it travels to non-followers:
- Retention: instant sound + "month 1" implies a payoff (month 12), 7 s loop.
- Shares: every expat and every Mexican knows the gas truck; "send this to the friend who just moved here".
- Saves: caption = "SLP street-sound decoder" (useful week one).
- Comments: "Which sound confused you first?" is answerable with one word.

### Shot list (7 s)

| Time | Shot / action | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.3 | Handheld, inside an apartment. Person on laptop jumps, stares at the window, confused. | Month 1 in San Luis Potosí | Gas truck jingle starts outside (recorded live) |
| 2.3–4.6 | Same framing. Person doesn't look up, mouths the jingle while typing. | Month 6 | Jingle continues |
| 4.6–7.0 | Person grabs wallet, runs out the door; camera holds the empty chair (same as frame 1 before they sat down). | Month 12 | Jingle + door slam; cut on the beat so it loops |

### Shooting notes
- Record the real truck from your street (original audio; no copyright issue). If timing is hard, record the audio first and act over it.
- Faceless option: POV of hands on laptop → hands stop → hands grab keys.
- Loop seam: start and end on the same empty-chair frame.
- Text: IG "Strong" font, navy on yellow `#FFCB05` box, centered at ~40% height.
