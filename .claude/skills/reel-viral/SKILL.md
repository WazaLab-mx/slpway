# reel-viral

Produces ONE ready-to-shoot 7-second Reel concept (plus one backup variant) for @sanluisway. The goal is distribution to non-followers: Instagram pushes a Reel outside the follower base when it gets high completion/rewatch rate, sends (DM shares), saves and comments in the first hours. Every decision below serves one of those four signals.

## Non-negotiable brand rules

- **All copy in English**: on-screen text, VO, caption, hashtags, CTA. The owner may brief in Spanish; the output is still English. Landing link = "link in bio" (bio → sanluisway.com/ig).
- **Audience**: English-speaking expats, digital nomads, medium-stay travelers and the curious "could I live in Mexico?" crowd. Identification must work for anyone who has lived in / visited Mexico, and the San Luis Potosí angle makes it ours.
- **Facts only**: any claim (price, date, place, distance) must be verifiable on sanluisway.com or an independent source. Never use prior-year event info. No "cheap Mexico", "$800/month" or "safest city" framing.
- **Never publish** without the owner's explicit approval of the final cut + caption.
- Keep the look of existing content (see Look & feel).

## Format constraints

- **7 seconds**, vertical 1080×1920, shot on a phone. Lo-fi = handheld, natural light, real places, no polished motion graphics. Lo-fi signals "a person, not an ad" and keeps the swipe finger still.
- **Loopable**: the last frame should flow into the first so the replay looks continuous (rewatches count as watch time >100%).
- **Hook lands in the first 0.5–1 s**: on-screen text visible from frame 1 + a visual or sound that creates a question.
- **Max ~12 words of on-screen text in total**, max 3 text beats. Readable in one glance.
- **Safe zones**: keep text out of the top 14% and bottom 22% (IG UI). Center column only.
- **Sound**: prefer original, recorded-on-location audio (native sound becomes reusable "original audio" = extra discovery). If using a trending track, keep it under the native sound.

## Look & feel (San Luis Way)

- Text labels: **Inter Black / Extra Bold**, navy `#011A42` text on **Potosí Yellow `#FFCB05`** boxes (or yellow text on dark footage). IG's native "Strong"/"Classic" font in a yellow box is an acceptable phone-made match.
- Accents: Paper `#F6F3EC`, Teal-black `#0F2229`. No generic blue, no gradients.
- Tone: confident, witty, warm, insider. Deadpan humor > loud humor. Never mocks Mexico or locals; the joke is always on the newcomer.
- Real SLP backdrops: Centro Histórico cantera, Calzada de Guadalupe, Tangamanga, neighborhood streets, mercados, a rooftop/tinaco view.

## Process

1. **Pick the theme** from the bank below (or the owner's brief). Test: can a stranger get it with the sound off in 1 second AND does it make them think "that's so me" or "I need to send this to ___"?
2. **Choose a hook formula** (below). Write 3 hooks, keep the shortest one that still creates a question.
3. **Script the 7 s** as a timed shot list (0.0–x.x s), with on-screen text, action and audio per beat.
4. **Engineer the four signals**:
   - Retention: hook in frame 1, payoff in the last second, loop seam.
   - Shares: a clear "send to" target (the friend who moved to Mexico, the partner who doubts the move).
   - Saves: utility in the caption (mini decoder/list/tip) the viewer wants later.
   - Comments: a CTA that is easy to answer in 1–3 words or an emoji, ideally with a reply loop ("I'll answer every one").
5. **Write the caption** (format below).
6. **Score** the concept with the checklist; rewrite anything that scores 0.
7. **Deliver** in the Output format. Do not publish.

## Hook formulas

- "If you know this sound, you live in Mexico." (sound/visual recognition)
- "Month 1 vs month 12 in San Luis Potosí" (transformation, implies a payoff)
- "Nobody warned me about ___" (confession)
- "POV: your first ___ in Mexico" (identification)
- "Tell me you live in SLP without telling me" (participatory)
- "The ___ only locals know" (insider curiosity gap)
- "Guess the price / guess the place in 3 seconds" (quiz = rewatch + comment)

## Theme bank (tied to sanluisway.com)

- Expat life moments: gas truck, street vendors' calls, tinaco/water, garrafón, "ahorita", tianguis day, power cut, rain in August.
- Food: enchiladas potosinas, tacos rojos, gorditas, mercado finds (link: food guides).
- Places: Centro at golden hour, Calzada de Guadalupe, Tangamanga, Real de Catorce, Huasteca waterfalls.
- Cost of living / home services (facts only, from site data).
- Events: current-edition only (FENAPO, Xantolo, Nacional Charro…).

## Caption format

```
Line 1: keyword-rich hook that restates the video (searchable: "Mexico", "San Luis Potosí", "expat").
Lines 2–6: the save-worthy utility (mini list/decoder, 3–5 items, 1 line each, 1 emoji max per line).
Line 7: comment CTA (question with a 1-word answer or "drop a ___ if…").
Line 8: share nudge ("Send this to the friend who…").
Line 9: "More SLP life hacks → link in bio"
Hashtags: 3–5 specific (#sanluispotosi #expatsinmexico #livinginmexico #slp #mexicolife). No hashtag walls.
```

Keep it under ~600 characters; IG shows ~125 before "more", so line 1 does the work.

## Scoring checklist (0–2 each, ship at ≥10/12)

| Signal | 2 = |
|---|---|
| Hook | Text + question visible in frame 1, ≤8 words |
| Clarity | Understood with sound off, no SLP knowledge needed |
| Identification | Viewer thinks "that's me" or "that's my friend" |
| Loop/payoff | Last beat pays off and seams into the first |
| Share/save | Obvious "send to" person + caption utility |
| Comment CTA | Answerable in 1–3 words, invites a reply loop |

## Producing the video (when the owner says "hazlo")

If live footage is not available, build an animated lo-fi version in code. Reference project: `C:\Users\sango\Desktop\san-luis-way-reels\el-gaaas\`. Copy it to `san-luis-way-reels/<slug>/` and adapt it.

1. **HyperFrames** (`/hyperframes` → `/hyperframes-core`): a single standalone `index.html`, 1080×1920, `data-duration="7"`, one paused GSAP timeline.
   - Doodle look: navy ink strokes on a Paper background, plus a `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` "line boil" whose seed is stepped at 12 fps with `tl.set`. Add a handheld camera drift (seeded PRNG) and a paper-grain overlay.
   - Beat labels are HTML clips. Beat resets use `tl.set` at each beat start.
   - Put the timeline script inline, because lint requires the `__timelines` registration in the HTML.
   - Never make a full-length opaque overlay clip (the layout check flags text as occluded). Make flashes short clips instead.
   - Repeated `fromTo` calls on one target need a `tl.set` baseline at 0 plus `immediateRender: false`.
2. **Audio (ElevenLabs)**: `tools/sfx.mjs` (sound-generation, at most 4 concurrent requests) and `tools/call.mjs` (eleven_v4 TTS for spoken or sung lines).
   - Mexican Spanish male voice: MexiTony `lRf3yb6jZby4fn3q3Q7M`.
   - Sound-generation does NOT produce intelligible words, so generate the words with TTS and the bed with sound-generation.
   - `tools/mix.sh` uses ffmpeg: a megaphone chain (highpass 420 / lowpass 3400 / limiter / echo), a bed at about 0.16, and loudnorm to −14 LUFS.
3. **Verify**:
   - `LANG_CODE=es node tools/asr.mjs <file>` checks that the words are intelligible. Without the language code, Scribe misdetects the language.
   - `npx hyperframes check .`, then `snapshot --at …`, and inspect the contact sheet.
4. **Render**: `npx hyperframes render . -q high -o renders/<slug>.mp4` takes about 30 s. Save `CAPTION.txt` next to the render.
5. Send the owner the MP4 path and the caption, then STOP. Do not publish without approval. For the Meta upload, if the file is over 10 MB, encode a copy under 10 MB.
